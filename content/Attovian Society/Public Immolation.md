Public Immolation is the use of [[Chomkar]] **Workings** to quickly raise the heat of an individuals body to the point in which they combust and self immolate until nothing but a pile of ash sits on the **[[Meyda-circle]]** where a person once stood. Many nations do not conduct Public Immolations anymore, however it is still reserved for specific crimes in the [[Ornsiire Empire]], [[Shen Dynasty]] and [[Zyclester Empire]].

# Zoherian
### Arm/Armed
Generally to impress excessive force
> [!example]+ Examples
> "This rock won't budge!"
> "Scoot over let me Arm it for you."
> 
> "She didn't push me, she ARMED me!"

# Elvish
### Cry It
Inferring the societal use of [[Tears of Memory]], this implies that one wishes to remember something or that something was memorable (perhaps whether they like it or not)
> [!example]+ Examples
> "That performance was brilliant, I'll cry it."
> 
> "You idiot, cry that for years now!"

# Gnomish
### Omu
A single word used in a number of places in Gnomish language that often implies completeness. Originating from their word for yeast it implies something is whole, full, or finished.
> [!example]+ Examples
> "How's the plans for the new bakery looking?"
> "Omu!"
> 
> "Omu?"
> "No sir, I'd love another round of steamed buns for me and my friend here."

# Drakonos
### Foru/Foruing
A non-curse but irreverent way to refer to sexual intercourse. Not always derogatory, but often used as such, or specifically in disapproval.
> [!Example]+ Examples
> "Those two are Foruing for sure. He only just rolled into town!"
> 
> "If your assuming we can Foru your quite mistaken."

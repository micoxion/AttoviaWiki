By and large Attovians use a positional base 10 numeral system using dots for nulls and 1-9 lines in a specific pattern to delineate a positions value. No one is entirely sure of the system's origins and while the [[Church of Hormus]] claims the shape of 9 represents [[Hormus|Hormus']] connection to Attovian soil, other researches posit that its creator was likely a member of the [[Crystal Guard]] and has a more generic **[[Meyda]]** inspired meaning.
![[Numerals.png]]
# Practical Math
Children are often taught math through the use of simple building blocks allowing them to actively construct the number of blocks they are currently counting. For those who need to do larger calculations on the fly the recent invention of the [[Shevboard]] has had massive success and is an extremely popular tool for accountants, merchants, foremen and anyone looking to manage lots of large numbers.
# Theoretical Math
Modern Attovian math advancement and study is based heavily on a number of fundamentals known as Yesod developed just a bit after the [[Eras of Attovia#Age of Nations|Age of Nations]]. Geometry and the more recent rise of analytic geometry largely dominate the current state of academic math especially with how they might apply to [[Fractalworking]] and advanced usage of [[Forms]].

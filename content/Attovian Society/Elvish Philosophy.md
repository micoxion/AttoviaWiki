The long lives of the [[Elves]] tend to determine one of three outcomes for the individuals of their race. Depressive debauchery, cruelty, and philosophy. Though many Elves poke fun at the fact that the third primarily concerns the previous two the number of Elves concerned with philosophical practice has steadily increased since its inception several thousand years ago. The current leading voice is [[Adlanna Caladwen]] who has garnered a significant following with her theories about desires as they relate to the **[[Soul]]**-[[Ogen]] relationship.

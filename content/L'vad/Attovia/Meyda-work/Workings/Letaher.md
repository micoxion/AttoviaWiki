Letaher while fundamental in the [[Fields|Field]] of [[Tiken]] **[[Meyda-work|Workings]]** is also exceedingly complex. Nearly all Tiken **Workings** are believed to have been sourced through [[Inspired Working]] thanks to the density of information they appear to convey and how continually unexplainable or reverse engineerable they are. Letaher is the holy grail of reverse engineering **Workings** for the [[Wizards|Wizard]] community. Doing so would unlock untold secrets about not only the Tiken Field but likely the body and **[[Soul]]**. Letaher's primary function once it is fueled appears to speed up the natural healing process of an individual. It is *not* as though it increases the rate at which time passes, for if this were the case it would be unable to cure mortal wounds which, if enough **[[Meyda]]** is provided, it can. The winding filigree like structure of the [[Meyda-circle|Circle]] when drawn has much to say about the workings of living beings yet it remains untranslatable. 

Letaher can be conducted by those with access to [[Meyda Intercession]], however the Field of Tiken is one of the only sets of **Workings** that are limited to specific entities. [[Dragons]] of all kinds are incapable of providing the proper interpretation according to legend, and it seems that any [[Corruption|Corrupt]] deity is equally unable to do so.

Leshnot **[[Meyda-work|Workings]]** are those which enact change on objects, materials and substances in the [[Material World]]. Most categorized Leshnot **Workings** exist as large classifying containers for the kind of Leshnot modifications which can be made on perhaps an unlimited number of materials. What most aim to do, especially the few that have been added to one of [[The Crystal Tomes]], is define, where possible, how to measure the potential cost of a given Leshnot **Working's** conduction on amounts of specific materials. The [[Fields|Field]] of Leshnot has the potential to be the most dangerous to the uninitiated thanks to the simplicity of some of the **Meyda-work** encompassed by it. The majority of [[Tzedef]] unfortunate enough to lose their **[[Soul|Souls]]** throughout history were certainly victims of Leshnot hubris.

**Meyda-work** is a surprisingly broad term on [[Attovia (Planet)|Attovia]] encompassing any practice that informs **[[Meyda]]** for the production of an otherwise impossible effect. This comes about through three named methods, the first two of which are common and well known to most of the continent. The methods are delineated *almost* exclusively by the way in which the **Meyda-work** receives the **Meyda** necessary to function. The way of instruction varies and even overlaps between methods of powering **Meyda-work** and will be discussed as they become pertinent.
# Transfusion
While not the most common, Transfusion is the first thought of by most when **Meyda-work** is brought up. This requires a [[Divisions of Existence#Soul Bearing|Soul Bearing]] entity to have a **[[Tethers|Tether]]** to a **[[Meyda-well]]**, a source of **Meyda** most often another, much more powerful **[[Soul]]** such as an [[Olam Beyn]] or [[Blood Dragons|Blood Dragon]]. This source can come in the form of a [[God Stone]] as well but *[[Zyclester God Stone|usually]]* means no [[Meyda Intercession]] is to be had. Transfusion effectively means that your **Soul** is the responsible party for pulling and processing the **Meyda** from its source. This taxes the **Soul** and leads to the symptoms underlined beneath the [[Meyda#Transfusion|Transfusion]] section of **Meyda**. 

This manifests in two ways: First the powering of a **[[Meyda-circle]]** via its **Meyda-well**. This usually is as simple as placing one or both hands in the proper circle where the **Meyda** [[Forms|Form]] has been drawn and opening their [[Meyda Gate]]. The second requires Meyda Intercession and involves informing *not* the **Meyda** but the **Tether** from which it pulls what your intentions are. This is most commonly done by drawing simplified **Meyda-circles** in the air and speaking the **Workings** common name or function in [[Abbaki (Language)|Abbaki]].
### Training the **Soul**
By default very few sapient peoples of Attovia are born with very potent **Souls**. Those who wish to walk one of the many paths of **Meyda-work** must first set about training and strengthening the **Soul**. Without doing so, anything at or above [[Complexity#Tiron|Tiron]] Complexity risks deconstructing the transfusing **Soul** entirely leaving the would be **Worker** a lifeless husk known as a [[Tzedef]]. Training is not difficult but often arduous, it is coupled with the tedium of learning **Workings** from [[The Crystal Tomes]] involving memorization first, then careful, regimented **Working** after. Pushing the **Soul** to grow without damaging it is delicate work that should be done under the instruction of a wise or well learned master. Each **Soul** is different and must be treated in kind.
# Transactional
Transactional **Working** is much simpler and cannot benefit from Meyda Intercession. It is the powering of a **Meyda-well** using another **Soulless** source such as a God Stone, [[Minor Stone]] or any number/denomination of [[Clips]]. If the Circle is drawn properly the moment enough **Meyda** is placed within its **Meyda-well** the necessary amount in solid form is demanifested and consumed, resulting in the desired effect. Transactional and Transfusion sources of **Meyda** are not "combinable" unfortunately, the lacking in potency cannot be bolstered by sufficient funds, a common sorrow discovered regularly by the wealthy and powerful.
# [[Old Workings]]
These are the most esoteric and strange, fitting into no proper taxonomy attempted by Wizards and researchers. These happen without direct influence from a single **Soul** Bearer, instead appearing to flow and occur based upon the thoughts of many. These have fallen away over the recent centuries as academia appears to destroy whatever functioning fabric of the world required for the Old Workings. The last two places where these are known to flow through the land are the [[Western Wastes]] and [[The Logalands]]. Known simply as the Old Workings largely thanks to how little they are understood. Attovian's of an academic mind have all but attempted to forget that they exist thanks to their research being seen by most as naught but folly.
# Other Strange Permutations
There is very little if anything that falls outside of these categories, embedded **Workings** such as used by the [[Swapping Sock]] or [[Flame Printer]] are just complex use cases of Transactional **Working**. The first drawing all it needs from ambient surroundings, the second powered usually by a Minor Stone or some other long standing source of **Meyda** not easily demanifested. Plenty of embedded **Workings** function only in the case of Transfusion as well. Most extraordinary weaponry or equipment falls into this vein, requiring its user to hold some kind of **Meyda-well** it can draw from.
# The Language of **Meyda-work**
When informing **Meyda** much of the symbolism used was discovered largely through trial and error, as well as inspired by some ancient religious practices of the [[Church of Hormus]]. Below are some of the basics.
### Concentric Circles
Concentric circles represent different objects of effect. One circle means nothing until you place it in or out of another. From their, the origin point of the circles, and their relative diameters heavily determine the following effect.
- World Stone: Concentric circles whose origins are identical with diameters in a ratio of 1:3 with a degree of error no greater than 5%.
- Atmosphere: Concentric circles who's origins are identical with diameters in a ration of 23:24 with a degree of error no greater than 5%
### Straight Lines
Straight lines with a degree of error no greater than 2% are used for two primary purposes.
- Connection: drawing a line between two entities that connects them within a magic circle implies the joining or connection of those two things. This could form a **Tether** if the two entities in question both have at least a False Soul. Or instead an alchemical result.
- Separation: Drawing a line between two entities that divides them implies the separation, often forced, of the entities involved. This can be used to sever a **Tether** between two “souls” or simply to purify water. Separating the h2o from everything else.

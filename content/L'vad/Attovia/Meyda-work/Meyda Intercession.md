> [!quote] An Old Wizard’s Saying
> A god may intercede on behalf of our more feeble of minds such that the **Meyda** may bless, and curse, all.

**[[Meyda-work|Workings]]** are intensely complex things. Besides [[Triflings]] there are very view **Workings** conductible through the errant medium of the sapient hand. For this reason the majority of **[[Meyda-work]]** is conducted through **[[Meyda-circle|Meyda-circles]]** where one can take their time to make each line and circle more exact. **Meyda** is fickle and requires nearly perfect instructions to execute its [[Meyda Functions|Functions]] accurately to the desired effect. Thankfully there is a solution for those who wish to conduct **Workings** via **[[Meyda#Transfusion|Transfusion]]** on the fly. It requires first, **[[Tethers|Tethering]]** their **[[Soul]]** to a [[Olam Beyn|deity]] or similarly powerful [[Dragons|creature]]. This connection via one of the five base [[Contracts]] is symbiotic in its nature. The powerful entity receives additional potency the more **Tethers** it garners, and the denizen of the [[Material World]] receives at the very least a **[[Meyda-well]]** of effectively infinite capacity and commonly the additional gift of Meyda Intercession. 

A **Worker** with Meyda Intercession no longer needs to communicate directly to the **Meyda** what it should do and how it should do it. So long as they can express to the higher being's **Soul** what it is they are attempting to accomplish it can effortlessly pass that on to the **Meyda**. The extent to which this is the case changes based on the **Working**, the entity one is **Tethered** to, and the agreed upon methods by fellow **Workers** as to how one should conduct said **Working**. It is a requirement that every **Working** in [[The Crystal Tomes]] be accompanied by a verbal string of reconstructed [[Abbaki (Language)|Abbaki]] with which to increase the success rate of interpretation.

# Deals with Dragons
[[Dragons]], [[Blood Dragons]] most specifically are obsessed with making such **Tethered** connections. Their mastery over **Meyda** means they can provide Meyda Intercession to a limited extent, enough so that most **Workers** would never require anything other. But their goal is less about potency and more about the hope of change. Their status as [[Mach'shavah Chofesh]] bereaves them endlessly. Each **Tethered** **Soul** is a potential touch point for the change they seek and brings them more influence and power. With enough **Tethers** the **Soul's** potency naturally grows thanks to the constant strain. Perhaps with enough connections one could finally break the shackle [[Graskoroth|Graskoroth's]] **Soul** binds them with. An afterlife might still be found for the cursed.

> [!quote] [[Kochavism|Kochavist]] Wisewoman [[Katsuko]] to an unknown [[Drakonos]]
> A binding line, that only kind of which we gladly fetter to strikes into the clouds and off towards [[Yahti]]. I could not be more certain young one, you are undoubtedly Moon-touched. Now pray your **Working** is of [[Yami]] and not of her brother bound to darkness.

Occasionally a Drakonos is presented with the gift of a **[[Tethers|Tether]]** that binds them to some entity on Attovia's moon Yähti. The origin of said **Tether** is usually unknown to the individual and could be either Yami or [[Hikari]]. Due to the obscure nature of **Tethers** there is no way by which to tell what entity your connection is to until you conduct **[[Meyda-work]]**. This has, in the past resulted in destructive and heinous disasters in which Hikari abuses **[[Meyda Intercession]]** to wreak all kinds of havoc and mischief. Thanks to several historically devastating events such as the [[Fukai Massacre]] a specialized **[[Meyda-circle]]** was devised that could derive the true form behind your **Tether**. 

One takes a blade of grass and places it at the center of a complex Circle, then the Moon-touched **Worker** transfuses **[[Meyda]]** to power said circle at its [[Meyda-well]]. Once powered the blade of grass responds by either wilting and dying demarking your provider of power as Hikari, or multiplying rapidly via its roots in which case the Drakonos is at ease for they have been granted a gift by Yami herself.

There are no recorded instances of a non-Drakonos Moon-touched individual. Many well learned [[Wizards]] believe it is possible, but that it would require someone raised by two Drakonos parents within the Kochavist faith. When it is considered that only one Moon-touched is seen on average per generation of Kochavist children the odds of this phenomenon gracing anyone but Drakonos would be a statistical wonder.

Originally the [[Rugadh Sliabh|Rugadhian]] population had no access to magic, built from pre-sundered earth they were unmatched in craftsmanship but were easily incapacitated by enemy spellcasters. [[Aoire Sléibhe|Aoire Sléibhe's]] last creation was the clan of the Sochraide. A group of all female **Meyda-workers** who became a Rugadhian vanguard against the hordes of the [[Goweh]] during [[Rugadhian History#The Waking Floods|The Waking Floods]]. In modern times the majority of Sochraide decide to alter their Oibrí, the organ responsible for their capacity for powerful **Workings**, via a ritual called [[An Lasc]]. This permanently reconfigures the Oibrí's purpose to that of creating new Rugadh.

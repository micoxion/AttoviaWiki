After the Bás Roghnaithe ends and the Rugadhian's [[Soul]] has departed for [[Tír Cheardaíochta]] their body is traditionally cremated. Once done so the final resting place of said ashes depends on several factors. Traditionally the ashes are joined with the Rugadhian's first [[Masterwork]], however if they were killed before one could be created and had no partner with whom they could be joined, then their ashes are joined with the stone at the [[Béal Brionnaigh]] so that it may benefit the [[Scríobs]] and [[Master Cruthman]] who's works pour from the mountain.

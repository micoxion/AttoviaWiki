A strangely vertical building that's first floor is entirely dedicated to checking visitors in and entertaining them. If one wants to eat and drink they must move to the second floor but not without first reserving a room. There are 4 more floors above each with 8 small rooms, and a final suite floor with just two large rooms.
# Owner/Staff
### Henry (Owner) - [[Krest]]
A Krest man who has dyed the feathers along his arms and wings bright orange and reds to attract more attention. He is boisterous and uses the rarity of his race to drive intrigue and make a profit.
### Master Ita Grúdaire - [[Rugadh Sliabh]]
The [[Sochraide]] responsible for brewing the alcohol in house and bar tends. Occasionally helps in the kitchen.

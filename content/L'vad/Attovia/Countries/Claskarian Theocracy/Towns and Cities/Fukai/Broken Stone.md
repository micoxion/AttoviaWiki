So named for the massive boulder next to the large squat building that looks like it was split cleanly in two centuries ago. The building here appears to have been made in a kind of modular fashion, sections of new Tavern being added on to the sides as time moves on. It has just one story but sprawls in an organic way taking up quite a lot of square footage. Thanks to the floor space an entire [[Gnomes|Gnomish]] bakery stands in her Tavern along with more traditional drink and food fare.
# Owner/Staff
### Eevi Karu (Owner) - [[Human#Amodians|Amodian]] 
A woman with an almost fiery red complexion compared to fellow members of her bloodline. She has a can do attitude and is happy to pay a group of Gnomes to take up a baking residency within her building which she expands as needed using a crew of fellow Amodians.

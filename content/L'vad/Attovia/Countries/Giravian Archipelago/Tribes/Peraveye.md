Peraveye sits on the far western edge of the portion of the [[Refike Zerin|Golden Reef]] that runs along the [[Southern Wilds]] where it touches the west coast of the continent. This puts Peraveye in a rare position of real danger thanks to the relatively close absence of the Golden Reef farther west which allows much of the massive southwest peninsula to develop a populace of [[True Monsters]] which find their way northeasterly often first colliding with Peraveye on their journey. Peraveye is therefore a very competent tribe when it comes to real life or death altercations, and their bad habit of killing the adult warriors of other tribes during [[Giravian Tribal Religion#Ritualistic War|Ritualistic War]] has led them to be an outcast of sorts. 
# Details
Population: 130
Adult Warriors: 30
Governer/Leader: Mehmud Mihemed
City Scale (in square miles): 1/2
### Problems
Peraveye sadly lost their last [[Ejderej]] a couple of generations ago, and a perpetual sense of loss hangs heavy over the tribe. Ejderej are *not* to be moved from their home islands if that island hosts a tribe, and those warriors who have been sent out to search for them are often subsumed by a tribe which invokes the rights of Ritualistic War on the small group or individual.

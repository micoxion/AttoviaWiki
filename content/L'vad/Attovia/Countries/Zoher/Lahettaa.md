When [[The Head Priest]] believes a piece of information or news is of great import he can "broadcast" a message to all the Bereaved of the [[Church of Hormus]] regardless of place and circumstance. This strange **Working** called Lahettaa has been a common phenomenon that the very first Head Priest was capable of. Like all of the many gifts and powers granted to the Head Priest thanks to their sacrifice to [[Hormus]] it does not appear that one needs to learn *how* to make use of the Lahettaa, it is simply something the Head Priest can and will do. In the rare occasion that one who proclaims themselves to be a Bereaved does not hear the Lahettaa they can be regarded with much suspicion. The biggest rumors and politicking in the nation of [[Zoher]] often revolve around who can and can't receive the Lahettaa, more so than the Lahettaa's latest message in many cases. Famously the Lahettaa has only been used in times of great duress, there have been several Head Priests who only ever used it once in their ruling lifetime and it was to proclaim their own abdication and subsequent passing.

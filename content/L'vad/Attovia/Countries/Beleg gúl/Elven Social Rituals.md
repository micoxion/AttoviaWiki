# Meeting and Parting
Two [[Elves]] who are close will often greet or separate after the "Tree's Clasp". This involves taking opposite arms and placing their elbows and forearms together so that they are perpendicular to the ground, then pressing thumbs together and spreading the hand. The final result meant to appear like and be an homage to the [[Ambar Tree]]. Those who are exceptionally close will grasp the middle of the forearm of the other Elf for a short time before separating.

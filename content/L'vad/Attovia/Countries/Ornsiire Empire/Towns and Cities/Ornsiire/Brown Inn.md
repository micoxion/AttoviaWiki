A building that sits in its own plot with a dirty brown roof kept polished and clean. Its semi unique color among the rest of the infrastructure is maintained for the sake of good business. The Brown Inn doesn't sell typical drink, instead only a clear spirit called [[Silka]] which they must import from the far north.
# Owner/Staff
### Inga Naumova (Owner) - [[Yewdi]]
Inga has her pouch wrapped having given birth to three daughters. She is a cheery middle aged Yewdi woman who takes great pride in her families business here.
### Varfolomei Naumova (Co-Owner) - Yewdi
Varfolomei calls himself a Co-owner but in many ways functions as a Janitor, handyman, and house-husband. He adores his wife and daughters despite finding them occasionally insufferable.
### Regina Naumova (Maid) - Yewdi
Regina is the oldest of the sisters, she takes care of cleaning the rooms and common area of the Inn.
### Liubov Naumova (Server) - Yewdi
Liubov is the middle sister and busses tables in the common area. When she isn't busy doing so she likes to draw behind the welcome counter.
### Anzhela Naumova (Resident) - Yewdi
Anzhela is still only 5 years old, and makes the entirety of the Brown Inn her personal play place. She is often carried around by her father as he tries to keep tabs on her.

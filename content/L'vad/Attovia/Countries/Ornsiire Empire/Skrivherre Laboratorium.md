> [!warning]- SPOILER CONTENT
> Those of the [[Ornsiire Empire#Skrivherre|Skrivherre]] who have sufficiently proven their usefulness to the [[Ornsiire Empire#Visare|Visare]] are granted access to the Skrivherre Laboratorium underneath the [[Visare Palace]] in [[Ornsiire]]. There are rumors that the [[Ornsiire Empire]] holds a [[God Stone]] in their possession, but no one outside the Visare's inner circle knows it is the glimmering blue centerpiece of their hub of research. 
> To access the research facility one must first gain access to the Visare Palace, and then its cellar. Inside the dark but well kept subterranean room the seventh keg on the fourth rack is false, and its lid requires the opener to have the necessary [[Meyda-mark]] to avoid tripping an alarm system that sparks a simple **Working** of flame that ignites the nearby barrels in the hopes of killing the intruder. Underneath the remarkably dangerous round slab of wood a ladder descends into the earth. Some thirty feet deeper than the floor of the cellar of the palace is a small compound of rooms and paths between that run extensively beneath the western hillocks of Ornsiire.
> # History
> The Lab's construction began in 2804 [[Era’s of Attovia#Time of Rest|Time of Rest]] just a few years post [[The Meyda War Ends|Meyda War]] in response to [[The Spear]]. Though the Ornsiire Empire had the strongest military force without doubt the potency of their [[Meyda-work]] was painfully, obviously, and significantly behind. The Lab was finished in 2812 ToR and the results of its research have largely shown themselves through modern quality of life improvements. The lifts on the [[Great Dal Bridge]] are one such example, but whispers in the palace court hint of **Works** being studied capable of unparalleled destruction.

Several years after [[The Meyda War Ends|End of the Meyda War]] the [[Ornsiire Empire]] and [[Kingdom of Helva]] met to formulate the Ornsiire Accords as a kind of good faith document to cement the peace between their nations. The accords while outlining a formal declaration of peace with longer vision than the [[Panjen Treaties]] spends the majority of its time codifying the agreed upon legal limit of **[[Meyda-work]]** and regulation of [[Fields]].
# Asur
The Asur **Workings** are those which are prohibited in all circumstances not even to be enacted by the ruling bodies of either nation. In the case that an individual is caught **Working** Asur they are labeled a [[Shota Worker]] and either executed, or exiled depending on the determined severity of their transgression.
### Asur **Workings**
Below are the Asur **Workings**. Some nix entire sub-fields of **Meyda-work**, others specific **Workings** for a variety of reasons but all link back to the safety of the people and the nation.
###### [[Necromancy#Auto-Necromancy|Auto-Necromancy]]
The act of experimenting and modifying one's own [[Soul]] is so dangerous to the surroundings and so rarely useful for anyone but the individual (if even that) that it was one of the first things added to the Asur **Workings** unanimously passing through and becoming law.
###### [[Ashem]] Impersonation of the Upper Class
The potential danger of someone disguising themselves through an Ashem **Working** as the [[Visare]] of Ornsiire or an important member of a Helvan [[Houses|House]] was not lost on those who met to create the Accords. This act was strictly forbidden despite the potential use cases.
###### **[[Tethers]]** Made to Deities External to Accepted Religious Entities
**Tethering** to a deity via means that are not common knowledge such as the [[Sundered Priests|Sundered Preist's]] [[The Tear|Tear]] is extremely difficult to derive on one's own. However, if such experimentation is *not* prohibited the potential cost could be devastating at locally apocalyptic scales.
# Mesukhan
These **Workings** are considered too harmful to allow free reign but too useful to be outlawed altogether. Therefor **[[Those who Work Meyda|Meyda-workers]]** who wish to conduct such powerful **Works** must procure a [[Mesukhan Permit]] from either Ornsiire or Helva.

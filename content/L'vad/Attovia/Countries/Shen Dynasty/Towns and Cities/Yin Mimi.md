Sitting about a dozen miles south of the northern coast and the western most border of the [[Shen Dynasty]] Yin Mimi has an unremarkable appearance. Simple wooden buildings dot the city proper with several stragglers spread out at the fringes. The settlement bustles with a regular feeling business. However, the longer one stays there the more confused they might become as to how Yin Mimi remains so wealthy for its size. The general population are quite rich when compared to other similarly sized towns and cities but the source of exorbitant income seems nonexistent. This strange boom in financial success and comfort appears to have been spawned the same year Chun Wu came to position of Overseer of Yin Mimi. 
> [!warning]- SPOILER WARNING
> Overseer Chun Wu came by a [[God Stone]] through connections with [[The Underground]]. How exactly she came to hold one of the greatest treasures known to the continent even via connections to The Underground is unkown to all but her and [[Huángdì Kai]]. She uses this God Stone to rapidly grow, mill and package an assortment of grains that are then sold throughout the Shen Dynasty which constantly has high demand for such produce. This commerce has helped Chun build Yin Mimi into a remarkably wealthy town which is quickly drawing new residents of all kinds.

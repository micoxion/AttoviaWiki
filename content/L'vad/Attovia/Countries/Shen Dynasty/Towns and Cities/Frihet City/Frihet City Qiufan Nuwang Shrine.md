Like all [[Qiufàn Nuwang Shrine|Qiufàn Nuwang shrines]] a pedestal supports a statue of a tall woman with gnomish features. Her hands are outstretched, her pose in general is very fluid despite the large number of chains enwrapping her figure. Traditional Gnomish dress of the ruling family can be seen through gaps in the chains and each wrist has one half of a broken manacle.
Qian Meifeng is the caretaker for this beautiful shrine. She's a busybody who is a strong believer in [[Qiufan Nuwang|Qiufan Nuwang's]] part in [[Gnomes|Gnomish]] freedom.

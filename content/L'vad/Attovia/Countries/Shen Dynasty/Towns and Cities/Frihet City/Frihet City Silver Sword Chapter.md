# Master
Vadim Sidorov, an extremely large, imposing [[Yewdi]], he has a surprising sense of humor for a Master of a [[Silver Swords#Chapters|Silver Sword Chapter]]. He is well beloved by the Students there and runs one of the most successful chapters of the [[Silver Swords]]. His long term goal is to increase demand for mercenaries in the area and get a second chapter opened in [[Vulkud]].
### Master's Assistant
Tagging behind the Master at all times is a small [[Theoir Sliabh|Theoirian]] woman named Tola Ilona. Any small or large task the Master requires which she can fulfill she will do her best to do so. Tola hopes that through this pathway she might one day take over Vadim's position or even greater be the first Master at the future Vulkud Chapter her employer desires so strongly.
# Admin Building
A building perfectly square to around 100 feet. In its center connected to the front entrance is a common area full of desks, chairs and tables often filled with Silver Swords planning for jobs or celebrating the recently completed. A simple bar behind which rests several kegs of ale and spirits is manned in two shifts by a set of gnomish twins Yun and Lim Leong. Across from the bar is the Job Board and the [[#Assistant Ledger|Assistant Ledger's]] desk where members can peruse, request and receive payment for jobs. Surrounding the common room are apartment like rooms where the Master and Assistants of the Chapter live.
### Assistant Ledger
Master Eabha Leabharchoimeád, a Rugadhian woman, is known to be one of the most efficient Assistant Ledger's in the entirety of the Silver Swords and has been rewarded more than once by the [[Administrative Core]] for the consistency and speed of her work.
# Enrollment Hall
The Enrollment hall here is a very long building, the front right side of the building when facing the main entrance hosts a small room with a desk that the Assistant of Enrollment sits at. The rest of the long building is a number of aptitude testing rooms. Each room holds a five by five foot block of stone, a straw dummy and a rack holding a short sword, battle axe and crossbow.
### Assistant of Enrollment
Rokuro is a black [[Drakonos]] man who's name means six, he comes from a large Drakonos family, a rarity, who live in the [[Shen Dynasty]]. He is relatively friendly, but takes no nonsense from applicants and prefers to do his job efficiently as possible. Rokuro is a practitioner of several different weapon disciplines and has a basic understanding of **[[Meyda-work]]** fundamentals including the primary [[Fields]] and the [[Forms]]. 
# Storehouse
Run by [[Jingshu Chao]], a massive single room building that has been split into two sections, a front lounge room where Jingshu sleeps, rests, and works when not doing inventory, and behind two massive sliding wooden doors the back 3/4ths of the building, filled with crates of supplies, explosives and armaments of all kinds. She also has a small section of the large portion setup as a display area to showoff any new exotic items she has found in order to turn a profit.

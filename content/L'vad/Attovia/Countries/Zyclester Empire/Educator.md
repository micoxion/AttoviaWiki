An official term in the [[Zyclester Empire]] that was created for the purpose of distributing the ratification of legal documents by government ordained legitimacy. Almost all Educators were originally simple teachers or those who's mental merit was recognized officially after [[The Meyda War Ends|the end of the Meyda War]]. Now the designation of Educator falls on official staff of the [[Zyclest University]] and those the University passes through the [[Educator Program]], [[The Table]] having offloaded the responsibility long ago.

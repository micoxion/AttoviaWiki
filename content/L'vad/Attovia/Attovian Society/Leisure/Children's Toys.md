# Kicksaaki
One of the easiest toys for a child or group of children if one has general access to [[Kasaaki]] in their region. Simply tie one of the little floating spheres out in direct sunlight for three days straight and the resulting compacted version is a much smaller green ball with thicker external membrane and an internal makeup almost exclusively liquid. The weight and feel of the Kasaaki in this state makes throwing and kicking it about quite entertaining and many adults will even participate in the elaborate games of catch and "pass the kicksaaki".

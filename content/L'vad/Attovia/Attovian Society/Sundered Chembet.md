When a [[Sundered Priests|Sundered Priest]] successfully completes [[The Tear]] they will be required to spend some significant time at a Sundered Chembet where they heavily study the [[The Life of the Sundered]], [[The Book of Nefiqaddis]] and the many sayings and writings of the [[Record of Head Priests|Head Priests]]. The amount of time a Sundered Priest spends at the Chembet is first stated by the [[Torn Order]] after an initial evaluation and may extend if their studies and practice move slowly.

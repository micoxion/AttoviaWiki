In the civilized core of Attovia the most common material used for clothing is [[Karva]] fur. Thanks to how easy it is to keep [[Karva]] at scale, the itchy cloth made from the [[Hajik]] plant is no longer commonplace. For the ultra wealthy, clothing that is both protective and comfortable can be woven from [[Spider Silk]] the demand for which continues to skyrocket for construction purposes as [[Spider Ranches]] have begun to multiply. Along the northern coast you will find most clothing made from the furs of [[Plavitel]], or at least fur layered over other typically sourced cloth.

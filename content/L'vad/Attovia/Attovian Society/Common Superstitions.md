# Thunder and Lightning
Thank to the ancient [[Human#Amodians|Amodian]] practice of [[Taivääntää]] many weather effects imply significant meaning to much of the Attovian populace. Thunder and Lightning are no exception, having some debated but largely consistent beliefs that circle the two interconnected phenomena. The lightning in its white hot brilliance betrays a seemingly good and happy thing with the follow-up cacophonous rumbling of thunder. Colloquially many refer to lightning and its thunder as the [[Demons|Demon's]] smile. Even those who worship [[Ukko]] see thunder as the result of his anger at the lightning's feeble attempt to light the dark world that he currently hides his face from.

# The Power of Worship and Prayer
Most who call Attovia home believe in the supernatural blessings one might receive from worship and fervent prayer. It is believed that the [[Olam Beyn|gods]] are capable of hearing each person in turn without confusion, and that they dole out some small gift and element of their power in accordance to their character. Even the simplest of coincidences is often interpreted as a sign or result of prayer and worship whether ill or good.

Historicity varies widely from group to group based on many variables no least of which includes the longevity of a given ethnicity's typical lifespan. [[Rugadh Sliabh|Rugadhians]] and [[Elves]] for this very reason tend to have the most consistent and ancient historical records. While the Elven records appear to be occasionally tampered with in reference to leadership and monarchs they are still believed to highly accurate in most other aspects. While the [[Human|Humans]], [[Gnomes]] and [[Drakonos]] keep written records, theirs are more prone to the two way leakage of fact out and religious sentiment in. The rest when left in their indigenous societal environments tend to keep records solely orally. Of the [[Yewdi]], [[Girav gel]], [[Semari]] and [[Biaban]] the last is likely the most well suited this form of record keeping as the exercise and importance of memory is a key element of growth and garnered respect among the [[Nomads]]. 

The only strange outlier that goes unstudied or considered by the surface world is that of the [[Ankan|Ankans]]. Web based [[Silk Dairies]] catalog the vast number of achievements of the current matriarch that rules the [[Goweh]] and are certainly filled with great embellishment, complete fabrication and self referencing "evidence".

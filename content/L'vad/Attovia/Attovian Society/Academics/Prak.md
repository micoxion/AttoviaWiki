One of the few products of joint cross-cultural effort by [[Attovia (Planet)|Attovian]] academics is the Prak. This is the basis of time measurement for the continent and is used by everyone but the [[Girav gel]] who do not appear to delineate the days any further than a morning, mid-day and evening. Everyone else utilizes the Prak and its sub-measurements to slice their days into 10 even segments each smaller measurement a tenth of the last.
- Prak
- Esprak (10th of a Prak)
- Meprak (10th of an Esprak)
- Esmeprak (10th of a Meprak)
No smaller measurements than the Esmeprak have yet to be needed by most researchers and [[Wizards]] but should they be required a system has been developed: An [[Mathematics|Attovian Numeral]] is placed to the upper left of the Esmeprak to inform the reader to what degree the Prak should be divided.

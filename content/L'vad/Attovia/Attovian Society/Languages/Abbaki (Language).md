It is believed by most [[Wizards]] that Abbaki was the first language spoken, and was the language of the first benevolent gods [[Hormus]] and [[Lielun]]. It is assumed that this connection to the world's origin is what grants the language power during **[[Meyda-work]]**. Abbaki as it must have once been spoken by the first peoples (largely agreed to have been the [[Human]] races) is long dead, and exists now only in its crude reconstructed form. After thousands of years of civilization rising and falling to the tides of [[Graskoroth|Graskoroth's]] rage many other languages splintered off and left it swiftly behind as a relic of the past.

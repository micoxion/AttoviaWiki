> [!quote]+ Excerpt from [[The Wise Conduct of the Crystal Guard]]
> So it was that [[Oskari Honkala|Oskari]] took a grove of [[Noren Tree|Noren Trees]] and built a small structure in which he placed racks for weapons, armor and simple beds using the leftover materials. He gathered fellow able bodied [[Zoherian|Zoherians]] and set them about training under the guidance of an old guard captain in search of purpose. He named it Chembet, a house of warriors, and taught those who could not fight to build and gather more and a small army began to form.

Chembet is a broad term used in several different contexts. It usually denotes a place where people are trained in combat of some variety, but has come to be used when regarding a place where individuals are trained by another in any specific discipline be it body or mind. This is not to be confused with [[Shulbet]] where people are trained in the mastery of a job or potential living.

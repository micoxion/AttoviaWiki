While the academic scene on [[Attovia (Planet)|Attovia]] has had a grasp on the physical relationship their planet holds with [[Lev'el]], their star, the more nuanced aspects of their cosmic position have yet to be discovered. The seasons are one such example. It is believed by most that the change in weather is caused by [[Graskoroth|Graskoroth's]] yearly attempts at breaking the **[[Contracts|Contract]]** [[Hormus]] holds with the land. Their tug of war goes on year long and each year Grask nearly wins out during Ratov, before Hormus retains his bond with the land before years end and begins it as strong as the last.
# Bright
Beginning on the 2nd of [[Zoher Calendar#Minui|Minui]] every year and ending on the 18th of [[Zoher Calendar#Nelui|Nelui]]. Bright is the warmest season of Attovia.
# Harvest
Starts on the 19th of Nelui and ending on the 1st of [[Zoher Calendar#Enecthui|Enecthui]]. This is the busiest time of the year in Attovian history, though progress begins to push work forward and reduce downtime this still holds true in most places.
# Ratov
Starts on the 2nd of Enecthui and ends on the 18th of [[Zoher Calendar#Tolothen|Tolothen]]. Ratov is the coldest time of year, and the majority of the continent populated by its people sees very cold Ratov's thanks to the continent's very northern latitude. The northern most places: The [[Shen Dynasty]] and [[Strana Gigantov]] see heavy blizzards and have societies built heavily around bunking down for the season and maintaining heat.
# Lifko
Beginning on the 19th of Tolothen and ending at the start of the next year on the 1st of Minui. Lifko begins the warming of Attovia as the northern pole comes back towards aligning with [[Lev'el]].

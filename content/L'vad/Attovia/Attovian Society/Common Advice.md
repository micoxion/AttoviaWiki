# Follow the [[Kasaaki]]
> [!quote]+ Popular Saying
> If your stranded in one of the many woods of [[Attovia (Planet)|Attovia]] and have lost your sense of direction just find a Kasaaki and follow it as it bobs through the forest on the winds eddies. It will lead you home.

There is a popular belief that following Kasaaki often reorients or even rediscovers one's position and place in a forest or woodland. Though not corroborated by any kind of research there are many second and third hand stories about wayfarers of the forest finding their way home after getting lost thanks to the simple but apparently profound motion of the Kasaaki.

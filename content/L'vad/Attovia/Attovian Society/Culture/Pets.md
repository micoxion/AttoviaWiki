Pets are not the norm among [[Attovia (Planet)|Attovian]] laymen. The rich and powerful often keep all kinds of pets such as [[Kaktaa]], [[Cirpi]], [[Pechi]] and [[Karva]] to name a few of the more tame examples. This does not mean that animal companionship is non-existent for the common folk, but it is typically transactional in some regard. [[Karva]] are kept for their fur to be sold, [[Nopine]] are taken care of to be mounts of great speed, [[Sika]] are kept for their strange milk and subsequent cheese (and are quite affectionate creatures, often bonding to owners quickly and strongly). In the [[Goweh]] the many [[Ankan|Ankans]] there keep [[Skitterling|Skitterlings]] for pest control and companionship, though the creatures terrify most surface dwellers. It is not a lonely world in regards to the presence of animals, but it is a practical and utilitarian one with just a bit of room for powerful bonds.

Though contested in part it is widely agreed that Vatorni began in the year 612 [[Eras of Attovia#Age of Nations|Age of Nations]]. It was started by a [[Zoherian]] [[Sundered Priests|Sundered Priest]]. She was a Leg and strove to pass on her newfound stability to those who sought it. Vatorni means strong tower in [[Zoherian]] and the discipline involves heavily drilling stances and how to remain upright after receiving blows. Additionally the discipline attempts to teach a kind of mental stability along side its physical aspects and now has several [[Chembet|Chembets]] in remote locations which are more commonly called [[#Vatorni Vasti]].

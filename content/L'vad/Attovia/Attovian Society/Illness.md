For the majority of the Attovian continent sickness is believed to have been brought to the material world by [[The Hebdomad#Asthéneia Lord of Disease (Eye opposite)|Asthéneia Lord of Disease]] and that even after his [[Demon Lords Caged|Caging]] it remains as a material function of his [[Soul#Soul Memory|Soul Memory]]. It is thought of by most researchers and learned individuals that falling ill is a kind of [[Meyda#Bombardment|Meyda-bombardment]] induced malady thanks to Asthéneia's Soul Memory permeating the material world. The healing brought by **[[Those who Work Meyda|Meyda-workers]]** of the [[Tiken]] [[Fields|Field]] is the most consistent remedy, but if no one capable of Tiken **Workings** can be found quickly it is often suggested to move individuals roughly a mile from their common residence in the hopes that this will give their **Soul** some rest from Asthéneia's corruption. Other natural remedies are used by herbalists and the [[Elves]] of [[Beleg gúl]] to varying degrees of success.

Outlier approaches to curing illnesses of all kinds do exist of course some of which are looked upon more poorly than others. Some anatomical experts have taken to removing the organs responsible for pains and discomfort resulting in a mixture of surprising success and catastrophic failure leaving their methods suspected by most.

There are occasionally [[Wizards]] who attempt to transfuse source less **[[Meyda]]** through an individual claiming such an action rids the **Soul** of the corruption. This is likened to the action of throwing water down a drainage ditch to disrupt and carry away refuse and filth that has begun to build up.

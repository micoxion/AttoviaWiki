The Shuzak were built from the stone of the [[Goweh]] by the [[Roeh]] as helpers in their Goweh wide engraving process. If any Shuzak still live they are unknown and have likely fallen into a deep slumber as their primary purpose has long since been fulfilled. 
# Appearance 
The Shuzak were constructed with precise engraving in mind, they had three arms on either side of their torso, and four legs arranged in a square pattern, each ending in angular talon-like spikes which they used to clamber about the large tunnels like strange misshapen spiders. Their eyes lacked the ability to focus at long distances but could see even the most miniscule details once within 3 feet. They all stood roughly 9 feet tall, significantly shorter than their creators but capable of efficiently engraving portions of the Goweh where the Roeh couldn’t traverse gracefully.

A group of [[Human|Humans]] who grow two to three feet shorter on average than their brethren. They exhibit stocky bodies that are oddly proportional to their different stature. Nammalian genetics appear to exhibit low metabolism rates and they are often rounder than other Humans on average though this does depend heavily on the lifestyle the Nammalian finds themselves privy to.
# Culture
Nammalian's like most other humans now embed themselves across the continent in every major nation and habitat. However they hold strongest to the traditions of their once homogenous culture than the [[Amodian|Amodians]], [[Tzelian|Tzelians]] or [[Zoherian|Zoherians]]. Many still claim a kind of loyalty to the long dead nation of [[Tugekoos]] and the vestiges of its once rich culture.

When one truly lays eyes on the wastes it might be hard to imagine a greater misnomer. A primal beauty exudes from the lush and fertile landscape, rolling hills of emerald grass flow into crystalline pools where [[Sudostu]] wade. Expansive swamplands, the primary buffer between civilization and the [[Semari]] people, make for dangerous traversal to the foreigner, but locals thrive in the strange biological hot zone.

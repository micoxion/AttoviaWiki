The Southern Wilds is a long stretch of land from east to west covering the southern coast to several hundred miles inland of largely uncivilized and uninhabited land. It butts up against the southern [[Grask Forest]], wraps around [[Darkmount]] and is partially populated in its southwestern stretch by small offshoots of the [[Girav gel]]. The Southern Wilds has a strange and storied past of many attempted colonization attempts throughout the [[Eras of Attovia#Age of Nations|Age of Nations]] and into the first two century of the [[Eras of Attovia#Time of Rest|Time of Rest]]. At first visit the land is fertile, and pleasantly cool most of the year round thanks to its proximity to coastal waters. Of course the [[Twilight Desert]] acts as a deterrent to colonization thanks to the difficulty of porting anything over the treacherous sands but if the [[Rugadh Sliabh|Rugadh]] are any example this shouldn't be stopping such a large swath of healthy and potentially lucrative land from being utilized. The conclusion come to by most who dive into the stories left behind by attempted homesteaders and mappers of the region is that something deceptively dangerous exists within the Southern Wilds. All accounts written in the region that have been recovered appear to cut off without mention of the cause to their disappearance but they have unmistakably vanished from the material world. The Southern Wilds for this reason are considered more dangerous than any other geographic region far more so than the [[Western Wastes]] or even [[Grask Forest]] thanks to the entirely unknown and consistent threat it poses. Currently all expeditions to the Southern Wilds have been heavily discouraged and no ruling state will fund what they like to call "A losers gamble". This was not the case for many centuries. Around 156-467 AoN there were countless groups of all sizes funded by a wide range of entities from governments to private benefactors. All that was left to find of any group that spent substantial time on their journey are long overgrown attempts at carving out civilization from the forest and lush plains. Half constructed cabins, derelict carts and the old bones of livestock left without caretakers. 

Speculation about the truth of the danger that resides in the beauty of the Southern Wilds revolves around a journal named *[[Terik's Account]]*. 
> [!quote]+ The Final Paragraphs of *Terik's Account*
> Luce finally completed the planter boxes and I've got some [[Kasaaki]] traps setup in the forest a few miles north of the homestead. We should be largely self sustaining and off of rationed supplies combined with poorly foraged supplemental goods. Two cabins are finished and I've taken the final cart-house as I've grown somewhat fond of the small but personal space. It does appear that my axe is getting dull as I find it consistently more troublesome to cut down and strip new lumber, but this was to be expected, I'm just not sure what to use. I'll admit in Ornsiire I'd have paid a [[Rugadh Sliabh|Rugadh]] to do the job for me, no matter how hard my father tried he told me a Rugadh's work would always last much longer. Altti told me he should be able to do an adequate job but still nothing like a Rugadhian's meticulous perfection. If he is done by tomorrow I should be able to finish stripping the logs I felled yesterday and can start on a cabin of my own. Perhaps I'll incorporate some portion of my old cart-house to pay homage to the weeks I've spent under its canvas roof.

This account is several weeks worth of daily writing and makes for the most complete piece of first hand experience within the Southern Wilds. It points the most to the idea of sudden and complete disappearance. The journal was recovered in a site that matches the descriptions of Terik's Account almost exactly up until its final entry. Nothing additional appears in the physical location that can't be found in the pages and the only incongruous thing is the absence of the "left overs" of three sentient beings.

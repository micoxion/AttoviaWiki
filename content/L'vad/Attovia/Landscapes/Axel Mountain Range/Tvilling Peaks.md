The Tvilling Peaks guard the southern most border which connects the [[Kingdom of Helva]] and the [[Ornsiire Empire]]. Their grand stature and the deep valley which cuts between them severing the end of the [[Axel Mountain Range]] has inspired many modern love songs, painters and artists of all kinds. Further enhancing their inspirational power is the [[Great Dal Bridge]] that has tamed their valley and left their innermost secrets exposed to all the world. Many of a sentimental mindset, or sculptors looking for stone rooted in emotional meaning take rocks and stones from the landscape of the Tvilling Peaks. 

One of the most well known love poems takes its name and impression from the Tvilling Peaks, though when though on for too long becomes a bit unsettling.
> [!quote]+ As the Tvilling Peaks - [[Balasar Myastan]]
> Our distance hurts me so
> Till on mind alights this thought
> That of a beautiful woe
> We mirror the Tvilling Peaks.
> 
> Like the stones of mountains fair
> I collect mementos sweet
> I delight in your auburn hair
> Our love like the Tvilling Peaks.
> 
> The Great Dal Bridge cuts far
> I shall travel down any a road
> To find you by night or day star
> I'll bring you the Tvilling Peaks.
> 
> Should I die without your love
> My **[[Soul]]** will ne'r forget
> I shall be with you forever above^[It should be noted that such verbaige implies the rare occurance of one's **Soul** remaining in the [[Material World]] as opposed to going to the afterlife.]
> A love as the Tvilling Peaks.

Auxiliary stones are [[God Stone|God Stones]] which have been artificially constructed utilizing a traditionally formed God Stone. The methods of creation are few and very recently discovered, it requires extremely detailed [[Meyda-circle|Meyda-circles]] and a sizeable God Stone to function as a source.

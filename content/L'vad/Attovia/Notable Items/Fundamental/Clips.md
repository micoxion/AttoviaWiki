Clips are the most common currency on Attovia. Found in all [[True Monsters]] the clip retains value due to its intrinsic magical properties, and the comforting knowledge that a clip represents one less goblin to haunt your fields. 

## Denominations

- Shard: Made from crushing a Clip via mechanical or magical force. Roughly 20 Shards make a clip.
- Clip: 100 Clips make a tat
- Tat: 10 Tats make a piece
- Piece

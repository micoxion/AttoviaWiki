A cosmetic ring with a surprisingly complex **Working** capable of creating an illusory effect around the wearer's hand. The effects vary but are often beautifully unnatural. The price of such rings is driven by the difficulty of their creation as well as their target buyers, the rich and powerful looking for any additional symbol of wealth and status.
# Typical Effects
### Flames
Your hand appears to be wreathed in flame at all times, the flames are real, but endothermic, and cannot burn anything.
### Shimmer
The hands are obscured by a constant sparkle. When looked at it is difficult to discern individual digits or form from the slightly overbearing shine.
### Blur
After images are left behind the wearers hand up to several seconds long that fade slowly.
### Pigmented
Colors the hand with a highly saturated hue usually requested in a commission. The very rich will have a Pigmented Glove Ring for every outfit.
### Highlight
Likely the most subtle of the typical Glove Ring effects. When looking at the wearer's hand a kind of halo effect warps light at the edges.

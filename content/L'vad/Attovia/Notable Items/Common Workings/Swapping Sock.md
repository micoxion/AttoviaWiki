When the Swapping Socks are worn each on a foot attached to a being with a [[Soul]] the bearer of the left sock can swap the socks at will by transfusing a very small amount of **[[Meyda]]**. This novelty of an **[[Embedded Working]]** holds two primary elements within its woven **[[Meyda-circle|Meyda-circles]]** that remain a mystery since its creation, estimated to be some time in the late [[Era’s of Attovia#Age of Vala|Age of Vala]]. Firstly it is as of yet unknown what pattern of lines, circles and [[Forms]] tell the Circles in question which foot the sock is on. Second and most confounding of all: The [[Fields|Field]] of [[Hanashem]] **Workings** (known as Teleportation in common vernacular) is one only theoretical, how and why such a coveted, mysterious power was granted to *this* particular article of clothing continues to baffle [[Wizards]] endlessly.

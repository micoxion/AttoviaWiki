A relatively new embedded [[Chomkar]]-[[Ashem]] **Working** used to lower the temperature of a 10 x 10 foot area. Requires a very small but steady flow of **[[Meyda]]** to function continuously, about two [[Clips|Shards]] an hour. It glows brightly during the process as it converts nearby heat into light.

An old **Working** used often during times of war or duress, but generally quite useful in any scenario one might need to signal from afar. A Natznatz refers to any object targeted by the simplest of [[Ashem]] **Workings** which causes the targeted object to emit blindingly white light. Usually a Natznatz is used in conjunction with a cover or [[Meyda-circle#Conceptual Jump Timers|Conceptual Jump Timers]] to make it flash, better getting the attention of those who might need spot it most.

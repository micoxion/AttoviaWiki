Thanks to the relatively recent addition of [[Spider Ranches]] to the Attovian economic landscape the continent has become obsessed with incorporating it into as many items and materials as possible. Nothing however comes close to the obsession with Spider Silk Bedding. It is warm, light, durable and extremely comfortable. Depending on the weave it can be used in the summer or winter months and it is smooth as satin. Greater still, many believe Spider Silk wards of illness if many kinds and this sleeping wrapped in the stuff could bring long term wellness to the lucky and wealthy. 

Caravans have been robbed by common folk, leaders have been swindled, lovers betrayed and families murdered all for a chance at that glorious bedding fit for a king.

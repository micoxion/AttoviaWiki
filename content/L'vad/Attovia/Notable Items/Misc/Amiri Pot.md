Created by the [[Biaban]] inventor [[Minu Amiri]] these ceramic vessels are engraved with a [[Meyda-circle]] and come with a small ceramic disc with an engraved [[Meyda-mark]] where the base vessel's **Working** can be powered with [[Clips]]. They are extremely convenient for those who have Shards to spare and though they lack any kind of temperature control they make up for this in their uncanny consistency. Forget needing to watch a pot over a fire, simply place your [[Hajik]] in water set three shards on the power disc and it will *always* be cooked to the same consistency.
Though quite efficient as far as [[Chomkar]] **Work** is concerned it remains quite cost prohibitive for individual homes instead being put to use in businesses and the homes of the upper class.

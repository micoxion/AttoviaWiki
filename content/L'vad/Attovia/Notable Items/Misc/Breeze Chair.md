There is a single functional Breeze Chair currently in use. [[Morgan and Son]] decided quickly to move on to the [[Breeze Cart]] instead after they realized they were perhaps attempting something beyond their means. The intention was to solve the problem of those born without legs since those born with legs that cannot be used can be cured at a [[Church of Hormus Temples|Church of Hormus Temple]] and those who lose the use later in life can utilize [[Tethered Prosthesis]]. The balance of a hovering chair to ease the burden of someone who might normally require a clunkier wheel chair turned out to be far more difficult than a cart. Now that the Breeze Cart has begun to pick up pace there has been talks between the father and son co-owners about returning to the previous project though it has quite a narrow audience.

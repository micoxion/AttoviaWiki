Elvish Mead was traditionally brewed strictly from the nectar of the [[Ambar Flower]]. This is still the case for any of the royal bloodlines within [[Beleg gúl]] but the increased population of the country and explosion in Elvish Mead exports has grown a subculture of brewers who use their own floral species. Elvish Mead is known to be quite rich; generally being combined with limited water and retaining high levels of its floral scent and flavor. It sets a high price^[Anywhere from 5 [[Chips]] to several Bits depending on where one is buying the alcohol.] thanks to the specialty traditions and skills required to make it correctly and has made many [[Elves]] of Beleg gúl quite rich.

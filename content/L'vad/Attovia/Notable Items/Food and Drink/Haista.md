A strange delicacy of the [[Human#Zoherians|Zoherians]], made of fermented [[Sika]] milk. It is exceedingly pungent and its name literally means "stink" or "reek" in the old Zoherian language. Difficult to find outside of [[Zoher]] thanks to its acquired taste it is common for foreigners to be dared into drinking the rancid smelling fluid by locals.

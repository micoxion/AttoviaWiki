One strict tenet of the [[Church of Hormus]] involves the complete ban of any worship in regards to a deity who is believed to govern time or fate. Both of these concepts fly in the face of strong beliefs held by the [[Bereaved]] that the [[Soul]] granted them by [[Hormus]] is in large part the gift of free will, and to even believe that another god could hold some sway over either of these intertwined concepts is believed preposterous.

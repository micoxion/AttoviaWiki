The Shema is an old [[Komer]] of the [[Church of Hormus]] that was eventually subsumed by the [[Mutaja]]. The purpose of the Shema was to put forth, and attempt to answer, questions regarding the legitimacy of aspects of their faith. This manifests as examinations of [[The Book of Nefiqaddis]], thought experiments, and questions about the true lives of [[The Head Priest|Head Priests]]. Though largely unknown, the Shema's absorption into the Mutaja was done very intentionally to remove the Shema thanks to the "reduction in [[Bereaved]] due to their dangerous candor." Though the Mutaja fulfill a similar role to the Shema, they present it in a less obvious and straight forward manner. This way, these questions and their potentially disruptive answers (or lack thereof) are found only by those already doing some extensive looking.

On the 14th of every month each [[Church of Hormus Temples|Temple]] of the [[Church of Hormus]] will hold the Sundered Sacraments, the only regular public religious service of the Church of Hormus. It is generally an all day affair that has two primary sections which follow a beginning speech or reading by the [[Sundered Priests|Sundered Priest]] in charge of the Temple in question.
# The Proclamation of Sacrifice
Each attending [[Bereaved]] who wishes to may stand in front of their congregated fellows and spout exposition of a sacrificial act they performed or saw performed during the previous month. There is also some space here to apologize to the community if one believes they have been selfish and have not sacrificed according to that sundered part to which they vowed.
# The Reconstruction of the Vows
This is to reaffirm and reconstruct the vows that each Bereaved has made to the given body part and its sacrificial aspects. Each vow is done via a call and response to a Sundered Priest of the same part if one can be acquired, or the next acceptable part. If only a single Sundered Priest is to be found they are permitted to do all seven vows but it is not preferred.

# Wrap up
When all is complete the Sundered Priests who attended this Sundered Sacraments service will remain until the 4th Prak attending to anyone who requests aid be it physical or spiritual.

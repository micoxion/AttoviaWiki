> [!quote] Of Water
> Of water I find a mirror
> Of water I find a life
> Of water I find a fire
> And never more of water am I
> 
> Of water I find a friend
> Of water I find a stone
> Of water I find a monster
> And of water forever I am
> 
> Of water my mother discovers
> Of water my father mourns
> Of water returned is my body
> And of water my family spurned

A lullaby sung by many mothers and children. Though a cheery melody pervades the common iteration of the vocal piece the lyrics are a somber reminder of the grave danger running water continues to possess on [[Attovia (Planet)|Attovia]]. In a hardly veiled manner the lullaby discusses the marvel a child sees in the running river or glassy lake. Drawn in by its majesty they are struck down by a [[True Monsters|True Monster]] and discovered later by their grieving parents.

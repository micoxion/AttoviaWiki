> [!quote]+ The first paragraph of *The Hand and Guidebook for they who Seek Self Experimentation*
> You are like me, or someone believes you are like me, or you do not yet know you are like me. We share in our likeness an understanding: To master oneself is the predicate to mastering all that comes after. I won't explore the philosophy of the matter instead implying that we see eye to eye already let me take your hand and walk you through the maze of thorny hedges until we reach the freedom fountain at its center. For in writing this book I was required to run into the many spears and prickles of the maze at full tilt in order to define its shape, and thus be capable of walking its many routes without scraping myself needlessly. That is this book. The picture of the maze that is the definement and dare I say enhancement of one's own Soul. Without such knowledge you open your existence to a scarring unknown with deep and terrible crevices wherein the **Meyda** that is *you* may fall. Keep your wits about you in most literal sense by adhering to the steps I have paved with ink and page.

The Hand and Guidebook for they who Seek Self Experimentation is the only thorough book on [[Necromancy#Auto-Necromancy|Auto-necromancy]] to have ever received copies and some level of distribution. It is extremely forbidden in all civilized countries including those much more lax such as [[Claskarian Theocracy|Claskaria]] and the [[Shen Dynasty]].

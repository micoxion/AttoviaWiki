> [!quote]+ Written by [[Melcthor Istya]]
> When I was a child I marveled at the great lightning strikes seen often over the [[Ambar Forest]]. Great shimmering veins lasting but a blinking moment yet realizing their solidity in cacophonous shouts. When I first saw one close I felt a tingling sensation over the full area of my body and the hair of the heads of my fellow [[Elves]] around me stood to sudden attention as though rubbed against dry [[Hajik]] stalk canvas. This observation though initially comical and childish has become a strange, recent obsession. I cannot help but believe the two phenomenon are related despite the near laughable scale difference. Whatever causes the mighty flashes and rumblings we account to godly fury may be strange **[[Meyda-work|Workings]]** of a power we yet understand. Not hurled by hands of the [[Overseers]] but something parseable by our minds and **Workable** by our humble **[[Soul|Souls]]**. What I seek to discover first is whether this is some strange machination of [[Ashem]] or an unknown [[Leshnot]] manipulation. For all we know it could be a combination of the two, another entirely or most exciting of all: A [[Fields|Field]] yet undiscovered ripe with potential.

A common tale told to those within the [[Louvi Polemos]] when in need of encouragement and a morale boost. [[Mihail Mylonas]], an ancient [[Drakonos]] man who was granted the first ever gift of Dorean. Though the myth is not consistent the legend always states a similar series of events:

Mihail is named a [[Hoplite]] at his [[Navexei]] and is shipped off to the front lines. He performs admirably under pressure and receives his name after only a few [[Time on Pafsitos#Seira|Seiras]] in the [[Trench Towns|Trench Town]] he defends. His rank was increased quickly to [[Enomotarch]] and his command performed far above normal metrics. 

Mihail's final act is what varies the most, whatever it was in truth it was a mighty feat of military strategy. Its result was the eventual migration of the Trench Town forward farther past the [[Light Line]] than any [[Fos]] trench had ever been.

> [!quote]+ The the New and Old
> We seek to grant the [[Fikcan]] with our sight
> May this form be pleasing
> May our gifts not offend
> May we one day carve ourselves from your heartwood

To the New and Old is a verbal component of the ritual the [[Semari]] people perform to christen each new depicted [[Balacan]]. Usually spoken by the creator of the depiction and echoed by the rest of their tribe before the first gift is laid at its feet.

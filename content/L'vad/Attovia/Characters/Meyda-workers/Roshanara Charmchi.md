A [[Biaban]] **[[Minstrel Worker]]** who works a circuit that runs a large swath around the [[Frihet Lake]] primarily visiting towns and cities in the [[Shen Dynasty]], [[Kingdom of Helva]] and [[Zoher]] with the occasional visit to the western slice of the [[Jalar Confederation]]. Roshanara found her way into her circuit without a master, instead taking over a place that was largely devoid of consistent **Minstrel Workers**. She left the [[Twilight Desert]] and her family of [[Nomads]] there more than thirty years ago now to study [[Wizards|Wizardry]], once she graduated from [[Zyclest University]] she turned down an admittance to the [[Educator Program]] and instead began searching for how her newfound knowledge might benefit the less fortunate.

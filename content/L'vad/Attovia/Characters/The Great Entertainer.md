A figure of legend that is still generally agreed to be real. Who or what he was is up for heavy speculation but most still believe he makes his home somewhere in the world, offering debauchery to a population in exchange for [[Clips]], gifts, favors and truly anything one might find valuable. Inevitably after a period of 6 years the business of The Great Entertainer will collapse, usually catastrophically and leave behind a strange hole in the hearts of the people there. Most believe The Great Entertainer is the moniker of the only [[Heralds|Herald]] of [[Trebulance]], others understand it as simply a title ascribed to a set of synchronous historical occurrences having no more substance than the patterns seen by the mind.

William is a hard, Utilitarian man who puts the good of the House above all else, even truly his nation. He will do anything within his power to maintain his Houses good standing on the Council whilst maintaining the façade that they are helping the nation. In an odd twist William's great effort to uplift his House at all costs is perhaps directly linked to his nation's success. Additionally his daughter [[Valeria Woodsoul|Valeria's]] position as Head of Operations has resulted in a number of highly beneficial operations being completed within and without the countries borders that have cemented its reputation as a country of order built on a solid foundation despite the real internal squabbling that never ceases.

Rong is the current matriarch of the [[Xun Gang]] and is very well respected by her family and the gang. She has continued to increase the gangs hold on their city and has developed a number of new important connections within [[Ornsiire]] that solidify the gang financially. The most recent of which being the Xun Gang's protection agreement with the [[Li Bakery]] which has mutually benefited the two families.

Rong is the daughter of the previous [[Xun Gang#Head Xun|Head Xun]], Zhou Xun, who she took the position from relatively early after her father was quickly painted as "soft" by the gang after a number of contracts and missing payments went forgiven.

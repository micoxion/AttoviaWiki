Of the three [[Ornsiire Empire#Skrivherre|Skrivherre]] who hold wealth and power in regards to pursuit of research and academia within the [[Ornsiire Empire]] Zalan is agreed to be the most influential. He is a [[Tzelian]] man of remarkably short stature who has put on substantial weight in his middle age and flaunts his wealth much like the rest of the upper class of his nation. No where does he do this more than his personal mansion in [[Ornsiire#Green Hills|Green Hills]] Ornsiire. Zalan has garnered an obsession with [[Chip Mosaics]] and pays exorbitant prices as a patron of this very specific artform. He pays especially high prices these days thanks to his new requirement that new pieces *must* incorporate at least one [[Minor Stone]] and of different colors in the case that more than one is added. Twenty of the walls of [[Hamli Mansion]] are decorated meticulously with original pieces made within the mansion directly. Each piece ranges in size depending on the size of its wall which it covers in its entirety, often filling the conjoining space with soft blue and purple light. The largest of these pieces covers the back wall of the Hamli Mansion great hall which is 24 feet tall and 80 feet wide and being made up of well over 70,000 [[Clips]] which swirl in dynamic patterns around a dull green Minor Stone meant to represent [[Lev'el]] and a purple Minor Stone representing [[Attovia (Planet)|Attovia]]. It took nearly three years for this piece to be finished by its artist [[Miyuki Shinoda]] who was paid in total nearly 40,000 Slivers.

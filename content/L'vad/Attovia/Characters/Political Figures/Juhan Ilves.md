Juhan the Crimson. A name well earned and well know on the continent. Brought about by the grisly displays Juhan Ilves popularized in the [[Visare Court Life|Visare Court]] that name displays well the tyranny that Juhan made his right hand during his time leading the [[Ornsiire Empire]]. A multitude of strong handed addendums to the [[Code of Visare]] were implemented that were extremely oppressive to the common folk of the empire. The already swelling corruption and inequality burst like a boil and covered the down on their luck with a new layer of disenfranchisement. The most well known of these addendums was [[Code of Visare#^612|612]] which set in motion a large swath of prohibitions to those of "lower born status" utilizing vague language, especially the word "excessive". This allowed the Visare and his cohorts to define in a moment when and where someone they deemed lower was trespassing agains the addendum and have them dealt with ceremoniously.

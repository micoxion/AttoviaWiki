A short [[Human#Nammalians|Nammalian]] man who hides rolls of fat beneath glittering vests and textiles others can't afford. His family line has presided over [[The Table|The Table's]] treasury for almost a century now, his great grandfather having been the first of the Stoneshires to take a place at The Table. His tongue is prone to the crass and his actions rash, but when one holds the coffers of a nation in their palm protests are strangled before they begin.
Salt and pepper hair betrays Bradley's march towards age and his brow wrinkles more than it did several years ago. His hands are almost always gloved in shimmery gold gloves that match his glitzy shoes. Bordered by the gold are velvet, maroon pants and a vest emblazoned with the flag colors that sits over a matching maroon undershirt.

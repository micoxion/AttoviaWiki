A [[Yewdi]] [[Sundered Priests|Sundered Priest]] of the [[Sundered Priests#Head|Head]]. Most well known for her participation in the [[The Prak is Decided|Deciding of the Prak]] and major efforts pushing forward education and academics within the [[Church of Hormus]]. She heavily opposed the dissolving of the [[Shema]] and worked hard (unsuccessfully) to return the [[Komer]] to activity.

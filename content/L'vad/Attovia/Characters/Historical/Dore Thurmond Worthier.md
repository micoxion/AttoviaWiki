The most mysterious academic figure the continent has seen in the modern era. Dore Thurmond Worthier is at least partially responsible for a shocking number discoveries and inventions purely via the written word. Their nearly robotic script is known well, even recognized at a glance by many, and it pervades every sphere of thought even the newer regions of philosophy. Reportedly as a student it was Dore who devised the prototype for the world changing [[Flame Printer]], and much of their work has been distributed by the same device. If they could be tracked down the [[Wizards|Wizard]] community believes them to likely hold a well of secrets and esoteric knowledge yet to be cracked by the most brilliant in the public eye. [[Percival Thornfallow]] often writes scathing essays regarding "Unworthier" for their lack of seriousness in the bettering of a world they understand so deeply.

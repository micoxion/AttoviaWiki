Very shortly after [[Ornsiire Becomes an Empire]] a meeting is called in [[Panjen]] of the greatest academic minds each nation has to offer. The [[Zyclester Coalition]] sent [[Zsofia Veres]], [[Ornsiire Empire|Ornsiire]] hosted the meeting and sent their first [[Ornsiire Empire#Skrivherre|Skrivherre]] [[Bela Eros]], [[Zoher]] sent [[Head Vladislava]], the [[Jalar Confederation]] sent a delegate voted on by the [[Jalar Union]]: [[Ott Meyker]], the [[Elves]] sent [[Sinwa Arpata]], [[Kingdom of Helva|Helva]] sent [[Fatma Leyla]], the [[Rugadh Sliabh]] of [[Daurk Brionnaigh]] sent [[Master Tadgh Leabharchoimeád Éabha]] and the meeting was remarkably graced by [[Sachiko]] the [[Record of Divine Stars#51st|51st]] [[Divine Star]]. With a base 10 system of numerals already the norm for most in academics it was a quick matter to split the rotation of Attovia into ten increments. According to Master Tadgh Leabharchoimeád the term [[Prak]] was put forward by Zsofia Veres coming from ancient [[Abbaki (Language)|Abbaki]] for "period of time". The divisions were further defined using additional borrowed words and syllables from the language of the gods.

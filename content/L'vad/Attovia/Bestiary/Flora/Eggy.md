A plant which grows a single large bulb like root which is most well known for its usage in the creation of [[Jolt]]. The Eggy is also used commonly as a symbol for rebirth thanks to its hardy nature and unlikely resurfacing after harsh conditions and natural disasters. The Eggy appears to be capable of using food stored in its large bulb to sustain itself for several years. The Eggy plant though burned, or left in drought can often regrow years later once conditions improve.

A vibrant green flower that grows in abundance in the [[Western Wastes]]. The petals of the flower can be used as a rich dye, and are involved in the coming of age ceremony for [[Semari]] hatchlings. Additionally its roots can be soaked in hot water to create a light and pleasant [[Yazil Tea]].
# White Yazil
A rare species of the flower often coveted by those in the central portion of the continent.

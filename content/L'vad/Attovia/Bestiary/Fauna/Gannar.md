Large beasts of burden originally domesticated by the [[Girav gel]]. They are very stocky creatures, with broad round feet, that taper into wiry legs capable of pulling very heavy loads. A single large horn juts out from under their chin which they use to scrape algae off of rocks under the waters surface which they then feed on along with seaweed as they wade in the ocean waters of the [[Giravian Archipelago]]. This makes them an extremely difficult animal to keep anywhere else due to the otherwise common volatility of coastal life. They drive a high price, and the overhead to keep them healthy even higher.

Giant Spiders are a strange creation said to have been produced originally by [[Graskoroth]] but without the same **[[Tethers|Tether]]** that binds and controls [[True Monsters]]. They are the only creation attributed to Graskoroth that has a relatively typical life cycle involving general survival tactics, self interest, reproduction and natural death. Giant Spiders have no centralized brain instead having distributed sensory processing in their large arachnid-like bodies that allows each portion of their body to react immediately and interdependently from any other part to great effect. Their [[Spider Silk|Silk]] is highly prized and is now harvested and farmed widescale in [[Spider Ranches]]. While their public perspective has begun to shift up they are still one of the most visually striking creatures that the average laymen might see during their lifetime. For this reason they are popular elements of scary stories, and the worst fears of the common folk. Children especially are often terrified of the creatures which tower over them with bulbous bodies and two extra sets of eyes.

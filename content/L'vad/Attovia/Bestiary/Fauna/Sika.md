Small thin mammals with long snouts which they use to filter nutrients and minerals out of soil. The female's can be milked for a very rich, hydrating milk. They are very commonly farmed in [[Zoher]] and many unique cheeses are made from their production. Most cheeses made from Sika product are extremely strong smelling. A fermented milk drink called [[Haista]] is made from their mineral rich secretion, and is an acquired taste that many swear by.

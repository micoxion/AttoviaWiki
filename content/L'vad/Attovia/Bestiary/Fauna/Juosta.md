The Juosta is a large quadruped mammal used often as a beast of burden when one needs more pulling power than a [[Nopine]]. If you need a cart or plow pulled a Juosta will do the job, especially if you live inland away from the few places [[Gannar]] are easily kept. Juosta are small but bulky creatures with thigh, calf and back muscles that bulge when at rest, they are capable of pulling much more weight than they might first appear and when passing by a cart laden with goods of all manner a Juosta is usually at its front. 
# Juosta Carriage
In the occasion that a number of people want to travel a moderate distance within the civilized portion of the continent they might hire a Juosta Carriage if such a business exists nearby. A single Juosta can easily pull up to twenty people if the cart it tows is large enough and makes for an efficient and lucrative business when one charges a fee for each individual. Juosta Carriages are most common in the [[Zyclester Empire]] and the [[Kingdom of Helva]] thanks to their position relative to other nations and the farther to the east or west one travels the fewer they find.

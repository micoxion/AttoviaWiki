The Virson company started by Ramsey Virson facilitates the transfer of messages using a number of Virson Stations in the following countries: [[Daurk Brionnaigh]], The [[Ornsiire Empire]], The [[Zyclester Empire]], the [[Shen Dynasty]], the [[Kingdom of Helva]] and the [[Jalar Confederation]]. Utilizing largely secret [[Meyvir]] **[[Meyda-work|Workings]]** the Virson Company charges a small fee to send messages of variable length to any other functioning Virson Facility. 
# Process
If someone needs to send a message at a great distance, especially outside of their home nation the best option is almost always a Virson Facility. Even if they need to travel dozens of miles it is often still much faster to use the Virson Facility than other more traditional methods. Inside a Virson Facility you wait in a small queue to use the Facility's Circle. Many Facilities have more than one depending on traffic in the area. When it is their turn they are let into the room where the **[[Meyda-circle]]** is sitting on a pedestal. They are guided by an attendant in the small room to place their hand onto the "Speaking Circle" the attendant also times the individual letting them know when the speaking time they paid for is almost up by the use of a small [[Astrostone Clock]]. 
# Limitations
Currently there is no way to "pause" a message and continue before sending in the case that the amount of [[Clips]] paid does not cover the full length of the message in question. The moment the client's hand leaves the Speaking Circle or the **[[Meyda]]** fueling the Circle runs out the message is transmitted. The client if they choose may rejoin the queue and pay once more to send an addendum to the previous message.

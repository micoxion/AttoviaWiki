Informal, but ancient all the same the Society of Wizards is a continent wide guild like organization formed to facilitate the sharing of knowledge. Thanks to the volatility of knowledge changing hands regularly within the Society the SoW is strictly invite only and it is more than an honor to become a member. Being a [[Wizards|Wizard]] of the Society means having access to vast quantities of private research and a share in the Society's rich pool of resources. Many banned tomes and even some thought lost to history are contained within the Society's library. 
# Center of Operations
The SoW meets regularly every five years, but can be called together under duress. The location of the Society's operations and the Society's library are entirely secret even to the members themselves. Only the Society Instructor is aware of its truth, and the method of entry involves **[[Meyda-work|Workings]]** unknown to the members.
# The Society Token
Upon joining the society of wizards a new entrant is granted a token embossed with the image of a [[Yalle]] on one side and a closed tome with a lock in its center on the other.
# World Builder Notes
The Society Instructor has been [[Terridor Throumehdown]] for well over a thousand years, he uses advanced [[Ashem]] workings to appear as though the Instructor changes in reasonable timelines.

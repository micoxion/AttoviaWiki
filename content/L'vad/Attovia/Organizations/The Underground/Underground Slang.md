# Biaolie
An [[The Underground|Underground]] specific piece of slang that denotes someone as a betrayer or backstabber of the organization.
> [!example]+ Examples
> "That Biaolie sold us out!"

This is also a semi-official term used by many [[The Underground#Triads|Triads]] to mark individuals either as targets for assassination or shame.
# Mande
A generally celebratory or positive piece of slang that implies a full purse of [[Clips]] will result in the action or scenario taking place.
> [!example]+ Examples
> "This operation will see us all Mande by the end of the week!"
> 
> "Mande! You're a genius rigging that [[Nopine]] race!"

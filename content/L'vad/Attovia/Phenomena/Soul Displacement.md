Though exceedingly rare Soul Displacement should not be taken lightly. Thankfully there is no known way for Soul Displacement to occur without very intentional outside interference. Instead one would have to conduct a failed or specific [[Necromancy#Auto-Necromancy|Auto-Necromancy]] **[[Meyda-work|Working]]** or be the subject of another's similar **Working**. Soul Displacement is defined by the decoupling of a **[[Soul]]** and its [[Ogen]] by relatively any means which has immediate and often irrevocable consequences. A displaced **Soul** immediately gets absorbed by the closest mass of **[[Meyda]]** whether that is a nearby [[God Stone]], the [[World Stone]] itself, or a much more potent **Soul** in very close proximity. Each has differing consequences that depend on the **[[Tethers]]** of the **Soul** in question. Being absorbed by a God Stone usually deconstructs the **Soul** entirely unless the **Soul** is **Tethered** to an [[Olam Beyn]]. In such a case the **Soul** maintains its shape thanks to the impressed image of a mind that an Olam Beyn can provide. Being absorbed by the World Stone often means a [[Ghosts|Ghost]] of the individual arrives in a potentially random [[Luumara]] *unless*, once again, they are **Tethered** to a deity of some kind which might drive them effectively to the afterlife, but without the manifestation of their body in the [[Material World]] which [[Bara Levatel]] traditionally encodes into the **Soul** after death. The Olam Beyn refer to these as [[Soul Shadows]] and at times, if benevolent, will give them bodies built of **Meyda** to retain a semblance of the physical body they were used to. The worst option is the absorption of one **Soul** into another. The joining of two **Souls** inevitably wreaks havoc on the psyche of both, and can result in the total destruction of one, and long term damage to the other. The bodies original **Soul** will tussle with the mind of its new resident causing many hallucinations, nightmares, sleep walking and in cases in which a more potent **Soul** is displaced into the body of one less potent the control of the body may be taken over entirely by the new **Soul**. There is no option in this scenario that results in a happy ending for either of the sapient individuals involved and only three records of this ever occurring have been documented. Each is a tragedy.
# Clarifications and Edge Cases
### God Stone Ogens
It is possible to emulate the function of an Ogen within a God Stone so that a **Soul** absorbed by it is not subsumed in its entirety. This results additionally in a more solid Soul Shadow concept where the **Soul's** impression of its Material World body is retained heavily including whatever it was wearing and had on its person last. Such a process can be used to carefully transfer a sapient mind from a Material body to a Extramaterial one and then safely back, but if left as a Soul Shadow for too long similar mental struggles will soon occur. Any realization to the fact that nothing about their "physical" form is truth in the Extramaterial World causes the **Soul** to rapidly devolve into insanity and transfers should not last longer than three Attovian days.
### Malevolent **Tethers**
It is possible to have an unknown **Tether** to a much more sinister deity who may play with your **Soul** upon its displacement. Such entities often hunger for displaced **Souls**, feeding off of the **Meyda** they provide directly. Thankfully they are rarely fed.

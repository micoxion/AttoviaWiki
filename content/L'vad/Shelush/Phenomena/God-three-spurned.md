The [[God-three]] are not known for wanton refusal of **[[Soul|Souls]]**, the afterlife, whatever it consists of, is a welcoming place. However, there are few places a [[Sojourners|Sojourner]] could travel where stories aren't told of [[God-three-spurned]]. A **Soul**, remaining in the [[Material World]], only perceivable by the **[[Tethers]]** which remain connected to the otherwise invisible **Soul**. For some reason it was denied its right to the world after death and remains, flitting strangely without obvious direction or intention. God-three-spurned are best avoided as they appear to **Tether** to anything in their proximity and the most common stories surrounding God-three-spurned are of the strange nightmares, visions and headaches experienced upon this undesired connection.
# Legaresh
Only a [[Soul Named]] is generally capable of Legaresh: The banishment of a God-three-spurned. However, the [[Whole-and-sacred-ones]] appear to be capable of Legaresh and have been for some time. [[The-government-of-steel]] has poured a smattering of funds here and there on a number of inventions which claim to one day be capable of Legaresh nearly automatically but nothing has come of it yet.

For the amount of time its [[Divisions of Existence#Soul Bearing|Soul Bearers]] have existed with culture, history and progress, Shelush's oceans remain in a remarkable dark area in academics. Certainly one major contributor is that the [[God-three]] appear to have made most aquatic life for much deeper waters than a society of their current technological scale can observe. While the [[Folk-of-brilliant-mind]] have cataloged a number of sea creatures these taxonomies are barely a drop in the bucket compared to life on land. Some trouble making thinkers have postulated the idea of a fourth society beneath the waves hidden from the original three. Such ideas are believed to be blasphemous by most and call for hefty punishments from the [[The-government-of-steel]]. How could the God-three break their contract and in such a catastrophic manner?

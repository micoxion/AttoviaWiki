The 1st of every [[Tri-hundred-turns]] is when [[God-the-first]] is considered to shift from being "asleep" to prominence. Each of the **[[Soul-bearers]]** believes they were created by God-the-first and are thus most potent and blessed during the month of [[Shelush Calendar#Steel|Steel]]. So each celebrates the new Tri-hundred-turn according to their traditions.
# [[The-great-metal-ones]]
[[The-government-of-steel]] mandates celebratory worship be held in [[Steel-churches]] or at [[Mechanical Shrines]] during the first week of the month of Steel. Special [[Prayer-plates]] are distributed by [[Fingers-of-the-first-god]] to any who have not memorized the proper prose.
# [[Nimble-of-the-sky]]
### [[Wings-of-blinding-glint]]
The rowdiest celebrations held this time of year are by these folk who throw parties and elaborate festivities in the streets of their cities and villages.
### [[Whole-and-sacred-ones]]
A recitation of communal [[The-powers-of-knowing|Names and Knowings]] by each tribes [[Most-knowing-one]] is the most common tradition. Feasts are regularly held, though the community generally eats in this way, so they are not nearly as of note as among the Wings-of-blinding-glint
# [[Folk-of-brilliant-mind]]
Regular monthly tests concluded long ago that there is no correlating increase or decrease in the cognitive ability of the general populace underneath the [[Fair-and-just-rule-of-minds]]. For this reason the changing of months and the "asleep" or prominent nature of the gods has been ruled as unimportant and not a "legitimate reason to reduce productivity."

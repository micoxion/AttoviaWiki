So named are the three deities who rule over [[Shelush]] in tandem. In accordance with legend a pact was made at the worlds inception in which each member [[God-the-first]], [[God-the-middle]] and [[God-the-final]] is each allowed one attempt at a given "concept". Create a **[[Soul]]** bearing species? Only one attempt each and thus came [[The-great-metal-ones]], the [[Nimble-of-the-sky]] and the [[Folk-of-brilliant-mind]]. This pattern continues for each and every thing present in Shelush, even its landmasses, though they have been altered by millennia of progress and great change. Each society claims their ancestry was created by God-the-first and subsequently their rivals were created. Each holds a different mythology in the mind of their people, yet they do agree on the rest of the God-three system.

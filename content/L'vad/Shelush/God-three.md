So named are the three deities who rule over [[Shelush]] in tandem. In accordance with legend a pact was made at the worlds inception in which each member [[God-the-first]], [[God-the-middle]] and [[God-the-final]] is each allowed one attempt at a given "concept". Create a **[[Soul]]** bearing species? Only one attempt each and thus came [[The-great-metal-ones]], the [[Nimble-of-the-sky]] and the [[Folk-of-brilliant-mind]]. This pattern continues for each and every thing present in Shelush, even its landmasses, though they have been altered by millennia of progress and great change. Each society claims their ancestry was created by God-the-first and subsequently their rivals were created. Each holds a different mythology in the mind of their people, yet they do agree on the rest of the God-three system. 
# The Divisions of Creation
While each people group disagrees on which of the God-three presides over which portion, all of them agree on the Divisions of Creation as proper encapsulations of each member of the God-three.
# Ocean, Mind and **[[Meyda]]**
**[[Meyda-leho]]**, thought and intellect, and the Ocean in its strangeness are under this Division. This does not include all things within the ocean, the divisions are more flexible than this. They are more ideological much of the time and many things in the oceans are attributed to other divisions if they align more with those tenets.

This Division is also believed to incorporate ice and snow and the frigid north and south. The mind's ability to push the body through such life averse environments is believed to align nicely with the Division's conceptuality.

Attributed to God-the-first by the Folk-of-brilliant-mind, God-the-middle by the Nimble-of-the-sky and God-the-final by The-great-metal-ones.
# Land, Power and Body
The continents, might and physical prowess are the philosophies falling under this Division. It is used regularly by The-great-metal-ones as justification for their actions, especially [[The-government-of-steel]].

Attributed to God-the-first by The-great-metal-ones, and God-the-final by both the Nimble-of-the-sky and Folk-of-brilliant-mind.
# Sky, Freedom and **Soul**
The firmament in its entirety, the freedom of will and choice as brought about by the **Soul**. These are the ideals of this Division. It perhaps hosts the least claim to created creatures and things, but its vastness is inarguable.

Attributed to God-the-first by the Nimble-of-the-sky and God-the-middle by The-great-metal-ones and Folk-of-brilliant-mind.

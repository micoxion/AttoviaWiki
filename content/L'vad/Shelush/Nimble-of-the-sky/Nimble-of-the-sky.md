Living upon the most interesting [[Home-of-safety-high|land mass]] that [[Shelush]] has to offer the [[Nimble-of-the-sky]] people are difficult to reach when in their natural habitat. They exist largely in two major groups of thought split down the middle who, in reflecting their homeland, are divided across a mountain range called [[The-wisher's-spine]]. On one, in the breezy plains of [[Crystal-grass]] and occasional [[Spine-tree]] are the [[Wings-of-blinding-glint]]. This group is very forward thinking, utilizing inventions and progress first developed by the [[Folk-of-brilliant-mind]] to augment themselves and their society. Wings-of-blinding-glint have left many of [[The-Powers]] behind in favor of technology and invention. Over the mountain range in the thick forests of [[Low-red|Low-reds]], [[Blade-singer-grass]], [[Long-frond]] ferns and [[Mother-tree|Mother-trees]] reside the [[Whole-and-sacred-ones]]. This people has held close to the ways of [[The-oldest-nimble-one]], maintaining rituals and traditions to enhance and cultivate The-Powers in their kind. 

Despite the ideological differences neither side holds disdain at a cultural level for the other. In fact many meet together in [[The-invisible-wall]] to share, swap and hold discourse over their ideas and to change from one to the other is not a shameful act. If there is one thing that both factions share in their thinking it is that growth of the self is paramount and should be sought after eagerly, followed to its conclusion and acted upon. [[The-great-metal-ones]] find their way of life frivolous in its constant ebb and flow; lacking a consistency they see as pivotal. The Folk-of-brilliant-mind think they are focused too much on the spiritual and philosophical believing they could have achieved much more remarkable things if they were not so bogged down in the pattern of their thoughts.
# Biology and Appearance
Nimble-of-the-sky are the most majestic in appearance of the sapient species on [[Shelush]]. Their six mighty wings are beautiful, the transparent membrane refracting light like a crystal. They host a single compound eye that takes up a majority of their head giving them a massive field of vision. Standing at nearly 9 feet when fully extended their legs host two major joints making a "Z" like pattern when standing at ease. Each segment of their leg is broken up into smaller flexible segments each of which can be compressed slightly on its own. Altogether this effect can result in impressive leaps which are how most Nimble-of-the-sky begin flight. Their body itself is also broken up into seven large segments, 1 for the head 2 for the torso and 4 for the abdomen and pseud-tail. In general Nimble-of-the-sky adults can fly for extended periods, several days straight if need be, but are effected heavily by air pressure changes at higher altitude. All Nimble-of-the-sky need some kind of aid whether it be technological augmentation or The-Powers to reach the Home-of-safety-high and it is such a journey to and from their home that cements one as having come of age.
# Life Cycle
A Nimble-of-the-sky is first laid as an egg by their mother. Where eggs are laid depends on the environments accessible features. In [[The-forests-we-know-and-name]] the Whole-and-sacred-ones lay theirs in the Mother-trees (the origin of their [[The Power of Names#Front Name|Front Name]]). The Wings-of-blinding-glint create [[Meyda-leho]] powered [[Nimble-egg-beds]] which warm and protect their soon-to-be offspring. [[Sojourners|Sojourning]] Nimble-of-the-sky seeking to have a child make due with whatever they have access to. Those on the islands in [[The-once-north-sea]] often build little sand nests on the ocean shore to allow their eggs to hatch in the ocean waters. The Moltings are all major changes during the life cycle of a Nimble-of-the-sky and do not represent the actual number of shed exoskeletons, most Nimble-of-the-sky molt dozens of times throughout their life.
### First Molting
Within a few days after hatching from its egg, the new Nimble-of-the-sky prolarva will go through its First Molting, after which its basic form can be seen but only two stubby wings, largely useless, are present on its back, and its exoskeleton will be a dull brown/orange with slightly translucent portions near joints. Before its [[#Second Molting]] it will be extremely hungry at almost all times and must be feed continuously until that time comes. As it is fed it will shed its skin many times steadily growing its wings out and increasing in size. Usually about half way through the First Molting a Nimble-of-the-sky child can begin to practice and achieve confident flight. Towards the end of the First Molting the unique colors of the child's carapace begins to show itself and is often the subject of its more long standing Front Name.
### Second Molting
During the First Molting a Nimble-of-the-sky only ever has two sets of wings at the most. When their final third appears they are considered to have reached their Second Molting. At this stage they have molted nearly 20 times and their growth will begin to slow if only slightly. They have several more feet to gain in height having entered the Second Molting somewhere from 3 and half to 5 feet, and will spend a few years doing so.
### Third Molting
The Third Molting is harder to spot, and usually is verified as part of a coming of age ceremony by an expert. Either a [[Most-knowing-one]] among the Whole-and-sacred-ones or an otherwise knowledgeable elder within the community elsewhere. The Third Molting is characterized by an exceptionally thin shedding that weighs substantially less than the previous, especially when taking into account body size. After this the Nimble-of-the-sky is considered an adult and will have shed their carapace nearly 50 times over their lifetime. Now they will shed thin layers very rarely simply to renew the bodies outer surface and repair potential damage over time.
### Final Molting
This Molting is always easy to spot, the single compound eye of the Nimble-of-the-sky is an opaque blue-gray color as it is unable to muster the strength to remove the old layer. This layer is thick, and it does not have another behind it. This is the final step in a Nimble-of-the-sky's life and it will last only a short few weeks. During this time they are treated with great care. All Nimble-of-the-sky society has many systems in place to facilitate very comfortable and safe living for those in their Final Molting. This is to honor them, but is also practical. Should their soft body be punctured or torn it cannot be repaired and they would die within hours or even minutes depending on the scale and severity of the wound. As they grow nearer and nearer to death the softening exoskeleton sags more and more become increasingly delicate. In the end most Nimble-of-the-sky leave the [[Material World]] on a bed of soft Mother-tree fronds, a bed reserved only for these in their final moments, softer than any other.

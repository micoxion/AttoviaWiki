The [[Folk-of-brilliant-mind]] prize nothing more than their intellect and they fear nothing more than the loss of its accumulation. The Extensive-historical-record-machine is their grand plan to make such a deplorable outcome as negligent as possible. It is capable of rapid retrieval of colossal amounts of information which it outputs using [[Crystal-storage-cubes]]. The Extensive-historical-record-machine has two primary methods of data entry, manual and extraction.
### Manual
Any citizen of the [[Fair-and-just-rule-of-minds]] who passes the proper competency and compatibility tests is allowed to input data and request data. Very few are rejected but the load is not an issue for the [[False Soul]] which makes up the computational functionality behind the impressive capabilities of the Extensive-historical-record-machine. 
### Extraction
When a Folk-of-brilliant-mind is nearing death they may choose to have their **[[Soul]]** extracted by the Extensive-historical-record-machine and have its total sum of experience added to the collection of data and history. Though once a distinguished honor this has become the defacto way to see oneself out of the [[Material World]] for Folk-of-brilliant-mind and nearly 90% of them will add their **Soul's** information to their culture's vast record before they die.
# History
The Extensive-historical-record-machine was a theoretical concept constructed only a few centuries into the Folk-of-brilliant-mind recorded history. For centuries after the idea's inception it simply wasn't possible when it came to the sheer power consumption such a thing would require. Not until the [[Mind-destroying-power-maker]] was put forward, and later successfully constructed, could the Extensive-historical-record-machine finally be built and used.

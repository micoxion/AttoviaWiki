When the [[Mind-destroying-power-maker]] was finally [[Mind-destroying-power-maker Construction Complete|complete]] the [[Fair-and-just-rule-of-minds]] proclaimed the first and only publicly mandated day off. The entire population was encouraged to participate in enriching [[Mineral Dips]] for the day, no workplace or business was to enforce regular hours at the risk of a hefty fine. Across the nation [[Folk-of-brilliant-mind]] were exiting their [[Legs-to-the-mind]] and allowing their lower bodies to bask in the natural springs that dot the landscape where they live in large quantity and proximity. 

No celebration of such scale, of such debauchery had or has since been seen among the Folk-of-brilliant-mind. The [[Nimble-of-the-sky]] would scoff at the thought. Debauchery assigned to simple relaxation? For a single day? Yet for a species as single minded, driven and communal as the Folk-of-brilliant-mind such activity is seen as vice and best kept to a healthy minimum.

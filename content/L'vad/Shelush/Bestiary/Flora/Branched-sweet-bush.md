These spindly little bushes grow abundantly in the northern region of the [[Severed-southern-continent]]. It has steadily grown in popularity throughout [[Shelush]] history as trade routes slowly blossomed and the [[Folk-of-brilliant-mind]] began to cultivate and export it in larger quantities. The sap of Branched-sweet-bush is where it gets its name having a high concentration of energy stored as glucose. Initially quite useful for the intellect heavy society of the [[Fair-and-just-rule-of-minds]] once the [[Nimble-of-the-sky]], with their more advanced tasting organs, got their hands on the stuff, they couldn't get enough. Branched-sweet-bush sap makes quiet the journey on a regular basis from the Severed-southern-continent to [[The-once-north-sea]] where the [[Wings-of-blinding-glint]] exchange all manner of goods for huge quantities of it.

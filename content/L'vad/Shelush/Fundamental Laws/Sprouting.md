At its most base description one who has Sprouted holds a **[[Soul]]** which bears far too many [[Contracts]] which favor another's **Soul**. Enough pull and the **Soul** begins to naturally protect itself by attempting to siphon a more equal portion of **[[Meyda]]** from Contracts in which it is favored. This begins to drain the **Soul** rapidly and that divide which draws the line between the [[Extramaterial World]] and the [[Material World]] thins for the **Soul**. **Meyda** leaks from it into the entity which bears it and their body often undergoes rapid symptoms of [[Meyda#Bombardment|Meyda-bombardment]] until it undoes itself **Soul** and all in a disgusting meltdown of biological matter and [[Hyper Meyda Density]].

The entire construction of the three name system in [[Shelush]] was invented and maintained to prevent Sprouting and it has worked for centuries quite effectively. Other hazards in the world exist that can cause such reactions from the **Soul** and body such as [[Where-it-all-will-go]], but while they bear the same name to the folk of the planet they are not identical phenomena.

Almost all things created on [[Shelush]] by the [[Abbaki]] who reside within it come in sets of three. There appears to be an agreement among the trio that permits each to have a go at an idea any of the other three might have. This results in a very strange kind of diversity. All creatures, plants and even some concepts have three variants that often appear related yet function in differing ways. The most clear example of this are the people and nations of Shelush: [[The-great-metal-ones]], [[Nimble-of-the-sky]] and [[Folk-of-brilliant-mind]].

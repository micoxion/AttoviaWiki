The planet of Shelush is... strange. It appears not one, but *three* [[Abbaki]] made their way to the same planet, perhaps of the same faction and decided to make a combined chrysalis. The resulting [[World Stone]] is *much* larger than normal as a starting point and the presence of **[[Meyda]]** is extremely dense. So much so that [[Contracts]] are formed with very little effort and **[[Tethers]]** are visible to the naked eye depending on their strength and distance. Thin, green lines are seen everywhere, connecting the population in a host of ways. 

Shelush is old but society has existed here only a short time relative to its inception, that is the *change* brought to it by the Abbaki which inhabit it. There is nearly only 4000 years of recorded society and history yet they have grown quite advanced in such a short time.
# Planet Status
Shelush has no axial tilt and a relatively even orbit. The result is a seasonless planet with relatively consistent weather patterns spanning the entire year. The equatorial band is very warm and the polar caps frigid with the full spectrum represented between (the only true outlier being [[Home-of-safety-high]] and [[The-Powers]] which hold and sustain it).

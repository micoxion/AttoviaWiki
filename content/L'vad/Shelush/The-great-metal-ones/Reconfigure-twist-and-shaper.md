This is a large device made up of a similar substance to [[The-great-metal-ones]] themselves. It is a large subset of modular parts which a [[Twister-of-metal-ones]] custom configures to fit the body of one of their own. Each piece aligns with tiny holes in [[The-pieces-of-great-metal-life]] and can be twisted, extended and enlarged to alter the individual member of The-pieces-of-great-metal-life to better align and support its surrounding connections. This full process often takes a [[Three-turn]] or two to complete depending on the severity of the affliction/injury. Twister-of-metal-ones are rare finds and are paid handsomely for their time, most of poorer status cannot find such help, and most either quest into [[The-holy-chasms]] or pray some lifestyle change will fix them with time. While some home remedies have exhibited small levels of success, nothing can match the deft work of those who work the Reconfigure-twist-and-shaper like an instrument.

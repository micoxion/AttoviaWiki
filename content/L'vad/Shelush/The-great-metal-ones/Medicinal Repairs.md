[[The-great-metal-ones|The-great-metal-ones']] unique composition sees the process of recovery, healing and medicine quite different from the [[Folk-of-brilliant-mind]] and [[Nimble-of-the-sky]]. While their biomechanical forms can recover naturally over time, certain lifestyles may inhibit this or even cause disrepair to pronounce itself unprompted. In such cases they must find something or someone to revert the problem. They may either search [[The-holy-chasms]] for [[The-fruit-of-life]] or pay a handsome price for direct treatment. A gifted member of their ancestry well versed in the use of the [[Reconfigure-twist-and-shaper]].

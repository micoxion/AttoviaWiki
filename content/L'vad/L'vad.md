The final, and only universe. Once the origin and home of the [[Abbaki]] where the [[Tree of Time]] set its boundless roots to support its great canopy. Now the only remaining *where* or *when* after [[The Collapse]] L'vad is filled nearly to bursting with all manner of strange life created at the behest of the Abbaki diaspora throughout its many galaxies and solar systems. The vast emptiness of space remains of course, even a full universe is a quiet and lonely place, but the odds of two **[[Soul|Souls]]** colliding of origins heterogenous is more likely here than in any of the infinite branches the Abbaki once cared for. 

L'vad was not always a "universe" in the way it now exists. Instead it was a sort of nexus, the home of the Abbaki and the roots of the Tree of Time. After The Collapse it was forever altered by the Abbaki in a desperate attempt to provide a final place in which existence could once again propagate at their whim. This process required the remnants of the Tree of Time's scorched portions which were pushed violently in all directions at the explosion of such a vast creation. Now at its center L'vad is the cradle for the roots of the Tree of Time which maintain the fundamental laws of physics.
# Galaxies
### [[Adomisk]]
Many Abbaki and [[Tzarakh' eleh]] have found their way to the Adomisk galaxy. An irregular galaxy with an oblong form that hosts much of the [[Koach Lehavah]] sect with several outliers such as [[Hormus]] and two other unaffiliated members taking up positions at distant systems where they have bided their time and begun the process of gestating new progeny.

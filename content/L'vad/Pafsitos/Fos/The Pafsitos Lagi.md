The ages of [[Pafsitos]] are known as Lagi and describe each perceived significant change that the [[Hikari|Platinum Queen]] herself has perceived.
# Oikogenia
A time before the [[Drakonos]] were created and [[Yami]] and Hikari lived in harmony.
# Echthra
Yami backstabs Hikari and attempts to destroy her beautiful garden of [[Platinum Orchid|Platinum Orchids]]. Hikari creates the first Drakonos and Yami responds with the [[Treloss]]. Dark and twisted beings akin to the Drakonos in little but vague form.
# The First Ptosi
After some structure is added to the Drakonos armies and the ladder of accolades and responsibilities is climbed the first [[Dorean]] are named and sent to the [[Attovia (Planet)|Axiom]]. The promise of [[The Platinum Queen's Request]] has been and continues to be fulfilled in its greatest numbers during this era.
# Polemos
What was originally many skirmishes and attempted peace talks turns to all out war after the [[Great Dishonoring]]. Organization increases and both sides dig their heals in by constructing large cities and bases with the fundamental purpose of creating, training and equipping each new regiment of Drakonos.
# Olotimos
The current age and the longest marked by the moment the two great lines of combat created a nearly perfect line around Pafsitos' circumference each backed by thousands of cities, forts, [[Trench Towns]], [[Exouse Artillery]] and their supreme leaders: two dragons of immense proportion each being impossible to see in totality thanks to their sheer scale.

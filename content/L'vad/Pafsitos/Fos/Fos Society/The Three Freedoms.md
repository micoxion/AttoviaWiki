[[Drakonos]] do not have "rights" so to speak. The idea is a bit incongruous to the foundations of their society and instead the Drakonos of the [[Fos]] have been given three freedoms outlined by [[Hikari]] herself. All other aspects of a Drakonos' life are at the whims of the great mechanisms of the Fos world. 
# The Freedom to Love
Drakonos who are *not* assigned to the [[Breeding Pods]] are free to choose mates at their leisure. While no official binding ceremony exists, some of the larger [[Trench Towns]] have their own little rituals to celebrate such a choice which rarely involve much extravagance or complexity.
# The Freedom to Choose
Once a Drakonos is assigned their position they are permitted to change where they work that position at will. At will in this case does of course mean with the proper procedure and paper work. Bids of Choice can take significant time to process, but they all do eventually come back. If a Drakonos wields their end of the bureaucracy correctly then their Bid of Choice is accepted no matter what.
# The Freedom to Die
If the [[Louvi Polemos]] is too much for a Drakonos they are permitted to act rashly or take matters into their own hands for the purpose of removal of their own life. If the choice in question has the potential to significantly harm or put in danger other Drakonos around them then they may be stopped and properly punished.

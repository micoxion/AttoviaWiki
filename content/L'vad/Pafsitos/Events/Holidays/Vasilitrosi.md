No consistency surrounds Vasilitrosi aside from its inevitability. At any time the [[Hoplite]] filled [[Trench Towns]] may be called to participate in its celebration. It is ancient, more ancient than the first Trench Town foundations which now support [[The City of Wonder]] and countless [[Breeding Pods]]. When the Trench Town [[Proicho Machina]] begin to blare a low frequency hum, great gatherings form under massive metal domes and small offerings are brought. Treasured trinkets, a favorite weapon, the severed hand of a [[Treloss]]. These gifts are siphoned of their **[[Exouse]]** to fuel [[Hikari|Hikari's]] most powerful works of destruction. 

Should your gifts be lacking, you are accepted in whole as replacement. Nothing is left behind.

[[Yami]] created the Treloss in response to [[Hikari|Hikari's]] constructed servants the [[Drakonos]]. However, Yami could not get them to behave as complexly as Hikari. She had somehow imbued her creations with true and proper **[[Soul|Souls]]**, something Yami was certain neither of them had the *capacity* to create. While the Treloss build and structure has remained static since their first generations, the mind which controls them is ever expanding. Each Treloss is a living organism in the same functional way a Drakonos is: Nutrients, oxygen, sleep, reproduction and death are all necessary and present elements of their existence. The only key missing component is a brain. Treloss were never built with heads in the first place since the structure seemed largely useless when not needed to hold and protect an organ they didn't have. Instead a singular organ that functions as both the [[Ogen]] and an organic [[False Soul]] allows for an instruction set to be processed and operated on. That instruction set has evolved over the many, *many* years the Treloss and Drakonos have been in conflict. While the False Soul can never truly replicate the complexity of a real **Soul**, the ever changing nature of Treloss behavior makes them a devious enemy to counter and react to. This accompanied with the lack of self-preservation in most Treloss makes them potent, and frightening fighters.
# Specialization
Treloss being built with specific instruction sets and forms are highly specialized in their respective skillsets. From the [[Kreas Farms]] to the front lines a wide range of anatomy is present depending on those tasks a given Treloss was made to conduct.

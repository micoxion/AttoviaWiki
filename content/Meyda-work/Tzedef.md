In the event that a **[[Those who Work Meyda|Meyda-worker]]** pushes their [[Soul]] to the brink they experience the common symptoms of over **Working** such as [[Meyda#**Meyda-work** Fatigue|Meyda-work Fatigue]] and **[[Meyda#Meyda-hallucinations|Meyda-hallucinations]]**. If they continue to [[Meyda#Transfusion|Transfuse]] **[[Meyda]]** past this point they are at risk of tearing their own Soul to pieces. If this occurs it is believed they cease to exist in truth, their empty body now wonders aimlessly or lashes out at random thanks to the now undirected firing of neurons and steady degradation of synapses. The most famous recording of the formation of a Tzedef is [[The Tzedef's Journal]], written by a likely [[Human#Nammalians|Nammalian]] woman who signs her entries with [[Liisa Rebane]]. 
The Tzedef's Journal is commonly assigned reading for masters of young or new **Meyda-workers** so as to impress the dangers of over-Working.

Hanashem is the theoretical [[Fields|Field]] of **[[Meyda-work]]** which involves the moving of an object or being through space in the material reality without that matter physically traversing the space at least in the traditional manner. Though long believed a possibility and agonized over for millennia in every [[Era’s of Attovia|age]], a recorded and repeatable method has yet to be discovered.

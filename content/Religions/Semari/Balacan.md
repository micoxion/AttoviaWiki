> [!quote]+ [[Aysu|Aysu's]] 2nd Saying
> We cannot make an image of the Fikcan, but each image made of its Balacan is like adding a leaf to the canopy of its portrait. In payment for our mirror and its offerings small favors will it grant.

Balacan's can be many things, a non-exhaustive list includes trees, stones, rivers, lakes, hills and mountains. They are considered the little souls present within each of these entities that in a larger sense are another face of the land's [[Fikcan]]. Balacan are each considered worthy of their own depiction and it is believed they will do small favors and chores for the [[Semari]] tribe that carve them and present offerings.

> [!quote]+ [[Aysu|Aysu's]] 1st Saying
> Listen to the words of the little creature. They see a world you cannot, they know a way you do not. Their advice should be regarded with reverence befitting the [[Fikcan]], for who else does its Will exist? We are but visitors in the kingdom of the wise.

Kismet encompasses an entire [[Semari]] ideology subset in a single word. The idea that small or simple creatures have abundant wisdom. Why this is the case is not always clear, and many [[Krest]] of the Semari tribes could not articulate it so, but the most sage among them might explain it thusly: 
Creatures of simpler, smaller stature lie closer to the heart of the [[Fikcan]] for they are its children. Offspring hold unwavering trust in their parents, and the unwavering trust the hare has for the land provides it spiritual sight and intuition. No matter how long a Krest might live, they will never be able to trust the Fikcan immovably, so they should instead respect and trust in Kismet.

Neladh trees are very squat things, reaching an unusual proportion between their girth and height. Their waxy sap of a light gold color is relatively tasty, and eaten as a treat by the [[Elves]] and [[Zoherian|Zoherians]] who have lived in its eastern area for much of their cultural existence. 
Their sap is used by some herbalists to create chewable poultices that can be preserved for long periods before application.

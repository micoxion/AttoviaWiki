Anything provided a [[False Soul]] can be largely quantified as an Animated Object. The common folk would disagree, siting the bad form of [[Necromancy#Profane Necromancy|playing with corpses and bones]]. But the only difference from a mobile suit of empty armor and skeleton is the packaging. 
The more familiar an object to the mobility sphere of a typically alive creature, the easier it is to provide it simple instructions. Otherwise too much unfamiliarity disrupts the enchanter's ability to form a proper image of how such an object would ambulate or gesture. 
# Creation Process
First the enchanter-to-be must find a [[Minor Stone]] suitable for the object in question. They then proceed with linking the two with either a [[Tethers#Strands|Strand]] or [[Tethers|Tether]] depending on the objects use case. Up to this point is well documented and there are some individuals capable of performing these **Workings** without consulting manuscripts. However, the encoding of instructions into the stone is still an unsolved problem among even the most brilliant minds that **Work Meyda**. Many of the **[[Meyda-circle|Meyda-circles]]** still used were originally reverse engineered or directly developed from [[Mordechai|Mordechai's]] notes and journals.

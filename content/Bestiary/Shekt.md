A small, characteristically sly mammal that hunts smaller creatures using simplistic traps. Primarily moving on all fours it has posable thumbs on its front paws and utilizes whatever resources it can in its habitat. The Shekt can be found in almost every biome Attovia has to offer, often passing on techniques and simple tools to their offspring and family units.

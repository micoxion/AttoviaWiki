A majestic feline that has prowled the floor of the [[Ambar Forest]] for millennia. Respected by the people of [[Beleg gúl]] as a kind of protector of the [[Ambar Tree]], and a ferocious hunter. Yallë have few kits in their lifetime, as they mate for life and if separated from their partner will never procreate again. 
Fully grown the top of a Yallë's back comes eye level to the average elf. Despite this impressive size and weight they make almost no sound when walking, and their eyes glow softly of [[Meyda]] giving them excellent night vision. Pitch black fur covers the body and a long puffy tail, the only variation appearing in the tufts of hair between their foot pads and around the rear.

The Komyen Grub is found most prevalently in [[Sondivar]] and the region around it, but populate any area below ground that hosts adequate amounts of natural coal deposits. The large grub tunnels underground seeking naturally occurring coal, when it finds some it secrets a chemical substance that breaks coal down into an absorbable liquid. The liquified coal can then be broken down into nutrient rich protein molecules with the aid of a symbiotic yeast which calls the Komyen Grub's digestive tract home.

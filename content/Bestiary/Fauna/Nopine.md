The Nopine is said to have been created by [[Trebulance]] as a kind of comical competition with Hormus own [[Juosta]]. It does look quite similar to a Juosta but has six legs, stands a bit shorter, and stretches a bit longer to accommodate for the extra set of limbs. Nopine are large enough to carry a single individual, and can do so around fifty percent faster than a Juosta, but their reduction in muscle mass means too much weight begins to wear on their stamina quickly. They are however, the fastest way of over land travel known to the continent, and are used most often for important, light deliveries.

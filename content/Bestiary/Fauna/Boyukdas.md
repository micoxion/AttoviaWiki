Boyukdas is a bird of enormous proportions that has not been found outside the [[Western Wastes]] for many centuries now. How many of these great fowl are left on the continent in their shrinking habitat few can say, but their size and reputation are legendary. Boyukdas mate for life, and lay eggs very rarely. If it weren't for their multi-century lifespans they would likely have gone extinct thousands of years ago.

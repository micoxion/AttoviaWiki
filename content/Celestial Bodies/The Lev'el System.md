The Lev'el System holds five observable planets for the current [[Era’s of Attovia|Era]] all of whom orbit the system's star [[Lev'el]]. All but [[Attovia (Planet)|Attovia]] and [[Yahti]] hold names that represent additional aspects of [[Hormus]] which were relatively forcibly popularized by [[Zoher]] in its early years. There are an additional [[Unknown Members|three]] that are yet undiscovered or unknown to the Attovian populace.

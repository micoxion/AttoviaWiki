World Hammer is a podcast in which I read entries to a daily prompt from world builders out loud and comment on the work. Its a great way to get inspiration and has encouraged me to read everyone's writing far more than I would have without the deadline in place! If you are interested in having your own world building or writing in your own setting read aloud then please join us in the [discord](https://discord.gg/9FvdCZmyA5) and look for the `#world-hammer-info` channel!

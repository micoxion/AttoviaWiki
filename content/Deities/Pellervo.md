Pellervo is a one of a minor pantheon of deities of the [[General Populace]] of [[Zoher]] inherited by the [[Shen Dynasty]]. Originally depicted with simple straw effigies which were burned in fields as Harvest Offerings thought to increase crop yields. Pellervo is now more commonly depicted as a muscular [[Gnomes|Gnomish]] man sowing seeds or wielding a small sickle. The continued pressure by the [[Church of Hormus]] has reduced worship for many smaller deities like Pellervo or [[Ukko]] within Zoher, but Pellervo has particularly taken root in the Shen Dynasty thanks to his connection to grains and thus baking. Pellervo is worshiped almost exclusively within the Shen Dynasty where small wooden carvings depict him in most larger cities where he stands behind a metal basin where crops can be burned as offerings: Seemingly a vestigial practice of the larger effigies in pre-[[Eras of Attovia#Time of Rest|Time of Rest]] Zoher.

Occasionally one may find small Pellervo carvings within the [[Kingdom of Helva]] on the property of [[Helvan Farms]], but outside of the Shen Dynasty his reach remains miniscule.

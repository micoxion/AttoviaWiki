George town is a small "sprawling" village. Sprawling in as much as roughly 15 buildings can do so. Upon its one and only street sits its inn, the mayors house, and Georges general goods store. Up the hill from the main street is the local farm which grows most, if not all the food which the town consumes. From there the rest of the village population is spread out around the surrounding area no more than 5 miles from the main street. Founded roughly 150 years ago by George Georgenson I (make page), the town has stayed relatively the same since those first 20 years of growth.

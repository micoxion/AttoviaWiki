Though the city's incline does not initially display this fact Saar Tower is the tallest building in [[Aear Gwing]]. It is the third [[Astrostone Clock]] tower ever created, and the last which [[Ai Chu]] had a direct hand in; The inventor visited [[Aear Gwing]] to personally oversee its construction. The tower's namesake comes from the [[Saar Family]] who paid for the landmark in its entirety. On its face this was a show of good faith to the city, however most locals recognized its not so subtle snub to the previous height record held by their rivals the [[Cundur Family]] who built the [[Aear Gwing Merchant's Guild Hall]]. In fact the Astrostone Clock Tower's peak sits exactly one Nach higher than the Guild Hall which stands on slightly higher elevation only two streets over from the Saar Tower. 
# Interior
The Saar Tower's first floor interior is exceedingly extravagant being the only floor open to the general populace. Promises of future tours showcasing the Astrostone internals have been around well over half a century and continue get pushed back thanks to the tower's initial budget having been entirely blown on its construction and first floor furbishing. [[Dominique Saar]] the current head of the Saar Family has made new, grand promises of tours and a completed second floor interior available as a space for the well-to-do to host and enjoy elaborate parties.

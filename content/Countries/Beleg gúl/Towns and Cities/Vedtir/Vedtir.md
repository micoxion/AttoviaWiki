Vedtir is the hidden blight on [[Beleg gúl|Beleg gúl's]] pristine image, the slaves they hold in secret here form a palisade of cheap meat against the final vestige of [[Graskoroth|Graskoroth's]] corrupt ocean hordes. Though called a city it is more properly labeled a prison, or slave camp. Just past the southern side of the [[Medfaug Wall]] sweeps the "city", roughly 20 miles latitudinally it is made up of guard houses, stone wall enclosures flanked by watch towers, warehouses and weapon stores. The greenery of the [[Ambar Forest]] is nowhere to be seen, its emerald promise hidden by Medfaug. Instead all that's visible in Vedtir is muck, blood, and the downcast faces of [[Elves|Elf]] and slave alike.

Once a watermill used to mill grain during [[Ornsiire|Ornsiire's]] time [[Ornsiire Pre-imperialism|Pre-empire]] when the Ornsiire river still gushed north to the coast. Now it serves as a tavern, including the creation of a novelty two story room which takes up the interior of the old waterwheel as it hangs over the dry river bed.

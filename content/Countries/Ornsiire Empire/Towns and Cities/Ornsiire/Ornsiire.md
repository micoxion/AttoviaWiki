# Buildings/Layout
Everything is everywhere in Ornsiire, bazaar's make their home in the square only a few feet from a tavern's front door. Derelict farmhouses and watermills make novelty attractions just across from residential blocks, and Brand Brigad outposts. Nothing fits in the quasi-logical way cities often form and come together instead a little of it all can be found most places.
## Shops
Where most cities see the congregation of merchants and competing businesses in the same general area, Ornsiire's odd history and organic growth has spawned a very different kind of spatial organization. Merchant's finding it difficult to purchase property and being unable to build new structures safely resort to setting up tents and lean-tos in one of the cities many squares. Little bazaar's selling an assortment of daily necessities, curios, herbal remedies, and whatever one might fit under a tarp are found throughout the city. Many businesses don't have catchy names for this reason unless they've been around long enough to get themselves a reputation and a sign.

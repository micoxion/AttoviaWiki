Master Bairrfhionn Cruthman, Bairr to most, is the most well regarded Smithy in all of [[Ornsiire]]. His clientele has grown and surged over the last two decades and now he only takes commissions, having no time to build up any kind of inventory. His shop that was before a place to display armor, tools and weapons is now a beautiful parlor with a screened off discussion area that hosts plush couches, several strong beverages, and a freshly stocked plate of treats.

The only tavern in [[Sondivar]], Pick's Hook only has three guest rooms, making the majority of its profit off of the Miner's they call regulars
# Owner/Staff
### Akbar Sadeghi (Owner) - [[Biaban]]
A tall, lean Biaban man with long faded tattoos of his heritage. His exceedingly long eyelashes have been curled up and clipped to the front of his curly hair forming a comical roll of hair that looks almost like curtains gently parted on either side of his forehead.
#### Leila Sadeghi (Akbar's Wife) - [[Human#Tzelians|Tzelian]]
A short, stout woman with a complexion on the darker side for Tzelian's, significantly darker than her husband's blueish tan.

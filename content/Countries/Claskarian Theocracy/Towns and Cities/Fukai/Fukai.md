Near the northeastern border of [[Claskarian Theocracy|Claskaria]] Fukai sits in the densest portion of the [[Yähti Forest]] intersecting the beaten trail that all travelers must use to pass through the area safely. It functions largely as a commune that subsists on the business of travelers. Though their population is low the monopoly on travel that this road creates warrants the heavy competition of four expensive taverns.

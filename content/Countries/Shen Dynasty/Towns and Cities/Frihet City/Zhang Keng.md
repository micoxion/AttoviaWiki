A strange landmark in the West Gate sub-prefecture. The remains of an underground sinkhole opening beneath an old wooden bathhouse. It descends steeply 20 feet below street level. The blackened stumps of furniture and structure not valuable to loot remains amidst the mixed cobble and granite rubble. 
# Details
The Zhang keng used to be a well known bathhouse with a well kept secret. A rival gang called the Qìpào used the place as their primary headquarters. When [[The Underground]] became aware of their presence the resulting destruction occurred several weeks later. There are apparently no Qìpào left, having been hunted to extinction by Underground members.

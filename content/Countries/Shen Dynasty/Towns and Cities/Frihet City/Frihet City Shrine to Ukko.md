Sits across the street from [[Frihet City Qiufan Nuwang Shrine|Qiufan Nuwang's shrine]] and is rarely noticed first. Hewn into a 4 foot standing log stands a small carving of a man^[perhaps 8 inches in height] holding what appears to be a mostly broken carving of a lightning bolt, the wood having been unfinished is cracked and damaged in many places. It does not appear that anyone in particular looks after this shrine, but [[Ukko]] is not poorly regarded by very many, and offerings do appear when clear skies are in demand.

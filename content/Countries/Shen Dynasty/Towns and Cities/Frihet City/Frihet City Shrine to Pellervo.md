Very close to the [[Frihet City#Old City|Old City]] sub-prefecture the [[Pellervo]] shrine stands at one end of a large crossroad with a beautiful fountain in the middle. Upon a wooden stand with a plaque that reads "Donated to the **Fu** clan by the [[Chi Kee Sawmill and Lumber]] family". It hosts a remarkable wooden statue of a gnomish man with bulging muscles who looks to be in the act of sowing seeds. 
The Chi Kee Sawmill and Lumber group has hired three individuals in shifts so that the shrine is always actively attended by an individual who may answer questions, dissuade vandalism, and clean when necessary.
- Mu Wong ^[female gnome]
- Ling Chao ^[male gnome]
- Ning Wei ^[male gnome]

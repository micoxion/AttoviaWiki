This small book handed to each [[Sundered Priests|Sundered Priest]] after [[The Tear]] is full of wisdom from some of the first Sundered Priests and [[The Head Priest|Head Priests]] in living a life worthy of maintaining your sacrifices blessing. After all to have properly performed The Tear and lose one's gift thanks to a needlessly selfish act or other wrongdoing leaves you with a permanent deformity and no way to recover the powers it lent you. 
> [!quote]+ Wisdom of [[Mauri Heikkila]] second Head Priest of the [[Church of Hormus]]
>  This grand vow you swear is to be shown to the world. It is our crest representing the most royal family. It is our badge and reputation for that which we have given. It is our scar and honorable, chosen injury. We are not to hide it, or shy away from it such as a washerwoman covers blemishes might they frighten away prospects. To do so is an affront to he who returned in kind to this grand action. Many Sundered Bereaved remain without their blessings a shame once unwarranted now carried with them till [[Sheol]] claim them.

[[Markus Lepik]] was a notorious general of the [[Zyclester Coalition]] during the height of [[The Meyda War]]. He is particularly special as he is one of the few important militaristic leaders who's logs and journals were made public by himself with the intended purpose of showcasing the horrors of **Meyda** fueled war as well as Zyclester's proficiency with it so as to aid in future proofing the nation he believed in.
> [!quote]+ 2799 Age of Nations - Atya 14
> With the [[Jalar Confederation]] now fully drawn out of the war our pressure to the north has increased dramatically. To combat [[Zoher|Zoherian]] troops however is to fight clay with shale, we are sharper but a filthy mess they leave behind. One moment a route, the next some remote miracle evaporates a score of our most potent [[War-worker|War-workers]]. Nothing remains, can't even send back a scrap of cloth or jewelry to their loved ones. That cursed [[The Head Priest|Head Priest]], no matter the distance he sticks his Phashing mind into the midst of our people and devastates my men in the name of his long dead god. 
> I'm grateful to any god that deigns to mark me worth consideration that these acolytes can't field more than a handful of [[Sundered Priests]] in a given battle. If they matched our War-workers in number by even a quarter we would stand no chance.
> My second in command here at the northern border fell with half her body scorched in the flames brought by an Arm's prayer. Linett was a trusted advisor, I will be hard pressed to replace her. No. In fact I cannot. It was thanks to her we won [[The Battle of Twin Lakes]] and it should be known thusly.
> We press further, towards Morus. I fear the density of those crippled weapons masquerading as people will increase rapidly as we continue. But if the Coalition demands we take their capital, then it shall be done. The rivers of Zoher will soon be choked with the blood of friend and foe alike. Those who are lucky enough to leave something behind anyway.

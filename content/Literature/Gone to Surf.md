> [!quote]+ Gone to Surf
> When my dear and darling wife expressed to me her woes
> We packed our bags and journeyed north for ice and ocean shoals
> The travel long, the road a mess but all along the way
> I found a secret happiness from watching every day
> 
> Her ruby skin a striking hue is painted by Lev'el
> If beauty could be bottled, to none she wouldn't sell
> When road connected old to new our home we furnished fine
> With wit and charm and hope and love and all of it was mine
> 
> A jealousy I quickly found for surf and seaside beach
> She clung to both with fearsome strength, always out of reach
> I lavished her with cherished presence, loitered for her glance
> Always she returned to them leaving every chance
> 
> I pattered on with grace and poise I would not be rebuffed
> But even I the loyal king felt loss of queenly love
> Our only shared emotion now infatuations pull
> Me for her and her for only krakens home most cruel
> 
> The night was dark I lie awake in bed of solitude
> The lover given all of me gone out to seek the flood
> A burning in my Ogen deep as something saps my mirth
> Loneliness tolls my soul for my light has gone to surf

A favorite of the famous **[[Minstrel Worker]]** [[Balasar Myastan]] and debated to have been his own creation. Gone to Surf describes the perspective of a loyal lover watching their partner descend into a complete obsession with the sea.

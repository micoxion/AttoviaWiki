One of the great Rugadhian warriors of old. Master Órlaith Laochra is touted as the slayer of [[Dia na Péisteanna]] with the use of a simple sling. Due to Rugadhian culture looking down upon destructive pursuits no Rugadhian historians appear to have recorded or passed on other accolades. It is however, noted that Órlaith did not die during the [[Rugadhian History#The Waking Floods|Waking Floods]], and spent centuries after as a fungus farmer, before going missing during the [[Desolation of Deep Home]].

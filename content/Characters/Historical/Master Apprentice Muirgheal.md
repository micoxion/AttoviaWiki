One of the twelve Apprentices selected by [[The Architect]] before his descent into [[Darkmount]] and creation of [[The Craft Mind]]. She was believed to have been chosen for her deep **[[Meyda-work|Meyda-working]]** abilities, a [[Sochraide]] of remarkable talent, she had engineered several important **[[Meyda-circle|Meyda-circles]]** for the processes of setting **Workings** to [[Rugadhian Steel]]. More notably however, is her attribution to the quoted line etched or carved into every [[Foirm Deiridh]] work. She is remarked upon as not only a genius Sochraide, but the [[Rugadh Sliabh]] responsible for the furthering of all Rugadhian purpose as it is known today.

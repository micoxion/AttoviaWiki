Among the [[Semari]] Aysu is believed to be the first of their kind to settle in the core of their tribal area known as the [[Gur Bulaqlar]]. He is considered the sole progenitor of their people believed to have been something higher than a humble [[Krest]]. Legend states the first Semari tribes were born of Aysu's relationship with the Fikcan of Gur Bulaqlar and he remained with them for many generations offering wisdom and guidance before he joined with the land and became as one with its Fikcan. The precepts that back Semari culture are tied back to many wise sayings attributed to Aysu. The end of Aysu's story also serves as example to the desired death of all Semari, to join the land and merge your [[Balacan]] with its wholeness.

> [!quote]+ [[Dore Thurmond Worthier]]
> I sought to find the strange fellow myself, and despite my best efforts he showed himself not. When I was in my study preparing my **Working** to be presented to the [[Educators Table]] he strolled from the flue of my fireplace, unfolded his [[Fractalworking Chest]] and proceeded to pitch me three small objects in exchange for a rumor, my favorite sock, and a debt of one story.

Gorb is fond of interesting, stimulating, and otherwise remarkable places and scenarios. He has the uncanny ability to present himself when his presence is most confounding, and trades his unique goods for riddles, puzzles, and anything that strikes his fancy. He appears to be a small [[Gnomes|Gnomish]] man who wears a pointy hat always flopping towards his left side. Strapped to his back is a Fractalworking Chest which holds his latest wonderous finds. To the few lucky enough to encounter the strange fellow he has blessed them with all manner of assorted objects both powerful and useless. No one knows anything about his past, has been capable of tracking him, or successfully predicted his appearance, but all have said he is kind in demeanor, generous with his time, and overall pleasant to be around.

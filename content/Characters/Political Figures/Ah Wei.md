Though small, Ah Wei is quite proud of being put in charge of [[Zhong Valley]], many others of the [[Wei Clan]] still shiver in the far north along the dangerous [[Shen Dynasty]] coastline. He is a kind overseer, but strict. Most in Zhong Valley appreciate his leadership, but most are generally apathetic as he has not brought about any significant change. The few who feel disdain Ah Wei largely do so thanks to the relatively obvious wealth gap he does little to hide. The only house that could be considered a mansion in the small town belongs to Ah Wei and the seven members of his staff.

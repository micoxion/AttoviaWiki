The great grandson of a farmer Rihaan comes from humble beginnings. His [[Girav gel|Giravian]] origins remain in his physical features and the slight way he accents his Rs. A legacy of his grandfathers thick accent.
He is the General First Class of the [[Zyclester Empire]] military and worked his way to the position over the course of three decades. Though a humble peasant when he first joined the training regiments his tenacity quickly marked him as a valuable asset to his higher ups. He could be relied on for almost any task and would push himself far past his limits to please superiors. 
As General First Class he now oversees the distribution of budgets and large scale training schedules.

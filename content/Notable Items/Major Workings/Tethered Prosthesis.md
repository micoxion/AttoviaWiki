A very new invention headed by [[Heimerdinger]] a now lost [[Fractalworking|Fractalworker]] of the [[Zyclester Empire|Zyclester Empire's]] Military Research Division. It was devised by himself with the aid of his mentor [[Tabitha Brentwood]]. The theory goes as follows. If one could mimic the "connection" between a **[[Soul]]**, its [[Ogen]] and the way in which the body's limbs are informed by it, then one could tether a mechanical arm to the **Soul** in question so that it behaved just as though it were the creatures original arm.
# Limitations
Currently the high level process requires an individual's **Soul** to recognize the [[False Soul]] embedded in the Prosthesis to allow it to function in a familiar way. If someone is born with a missing limb or extremity there is not currently a way to utilize the Tethered Prosthesis to aid them.

Only two individuals know the order of the [[Forms]] required to construct the proper False Soul that may interface as a missing part. Heimerdinger is missing and Tabitha appears to be preoccupied with finding him so progress on production has halted.

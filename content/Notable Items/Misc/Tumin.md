A large oblong red fruit which grows on a deciduous tree, with oval leaves that come slowly to a point. Before fruiting the blossoms are a soft yellow, and the golden bloom is most often seen in early [[Zoher Calendar#Tolothen|Tolothen]] and is called the Tumin Dawn.

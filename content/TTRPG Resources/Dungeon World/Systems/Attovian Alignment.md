###### Pure
- Put yourself in harms way for another
- Cleansed corruption
- Show mercy
- Give something up for the greater good
- Avoid or dissolve a potential violent conflict
###### Apathetic
- Ignored happenings in pursuit of self interest
- Gained new knowledge or treasure
- Escaped personal injury no matter the cost
###### Corrupt
- Destroyed a piece of civilization
- Discovered dangerous secrets
- Insighted chaos
- Got away with a horrible act
- Turned another corrupt

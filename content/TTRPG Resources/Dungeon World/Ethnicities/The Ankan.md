The Ankan people are the rarest of all ethnicities above ground, but are the sole members of culture and society beneath the surface where they dwell in the winding tunnels of the [[Goweh]]. Eight legs attached to a spider-like thorax and a humanoid torso that sprouts from the thorax's front. Eight thin eyes and a false mouth that hides an unpleasantly mandible filled mouth.
# Horrifying Visage
No matter what you do you are unnerving to those around you. Anytime you would Parley with someone using fear or force as leverage take +1, anytime you would Parley with any other leverage besides money take -1
# Gendered Forms
The caste of the Ankan's of the Goweh is determined first by gender. Matriarchal society has made for large female Ankan's and small agile males. If you choose to be a female gain the Seamstress move, if you choose to be a male gain the infiltrator move.
### Seamstress
Anytime you **wish to create something with your web** roll+dex. On a 10+ you create what you want within reason. On a 7-9 you create something that will function for this purpose but it will either only work once, or has a major flaw. 
### Infiltrator
When you **attempt to sneak into a place you should not be** roll+dex. On a 10+ you are inside and no one is the wiser, even your fellow party members, you may take +1 ongoing while in this location. On a 7-9 you are inside but you are in a spot and must make a difficult decision of the GMs choice.

# An Obsessive Mind
Choose a craft from the list below or work out your own with the GM, anytime you do anything in line with the pursuit of this craft take +1. If you spend more than a day without pursuing this craft in some way take -1 ongoing until you do so.
- Cruthman: All things of the forge and smithy
- Leabharchoimeád: Recorders, memorizers and distributers of history. Bookkeepers in a sense, of the past.
- Oibhachta: An unofficial job title claimed by the few Rugadh who wish to subject their life's work to the pursuit of **Meyda-work**. This will put you at odds with others of your ethnicity who take it as a snub to your heritage.
- Chlochair: Masonry, stonework and anything involving a hammer and chisel on hard minerals.
- Contasóir: Accountants, business owners, they love to work with money and figure out economic systems.
- Grúdaire: Brewers of alcohol, tinctures, potions and balms.

# Split-mind
Your brain has two fully separate hemispheres without the typical corpus callosum found in other sentient species. Because your mind is split you do not need to sleep, instead resting one side of the brain when it tires allowing the other to take over. Additionally Elves are capable of using the separate processing portions of their brain to hide secrets in one half and tell the the truth from the other. Anytime you would Parley of Defy Danger in a way that incorporates lying or hiding truths you get +1 to the roll.
# The Ambar Tree
You innately have a **Tether** to the Ambar Tree. You may take moves which require **Meyda-wells** thanks to this connection. This connection can be severed against your will by [[The Court in Ambar]] if they deem you [[Hekil]].

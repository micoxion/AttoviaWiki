The Warmonger does not yearn for war they instead see its inevitability. When the storm clouds break over the horizon you take the Sika indoors, ensure all children and old folk are accounted for, before you face the weathers wrath. So too does a Warmonger prepare. You seek connections everywhere, favorable or otherwise, if it is aid or allyship it is useful to you. Power does not come from raw might, or knives between shoulder blades. Power comes from the web you weave across the continent. 
# Stats
Your maximum HP is 10+Constitution
Your base damage is d10
# Starting Moves
### Foresight
When you **set out on a job or mission in which you have time to prepare** choose two preparedness benefits:
- Honed steel: +1d6 to attacks made with your weapons
- Oiled and Polished: +1 armor
- There's a map for that: No need to search for or way find to your destination if you otherwise would need to.
- I know a guy: Choose an item you already have in your inventory and add 1d4 more. (This item should be something you could have a supplier for, talk with your GM about which item makes sense)
- Warpaint: You make yourself look fearsome, +1 ongoing to parley until this mission ends or your prep no longer makes sense.
### Tug at the Threads
When you **seek to use an ally who is part of your web** roll+CHA on a 10+ take both, on a 7-9 choose 1:
- You get exactly what you want
- You don't owe them a favor in return
### I Can Use This
When you go to add something to your inventory, if you can manage to make that item worn in a reasonable manner (as opposed to packed) you may mark its load as 0.
### Forge a Contract
When you **attempt to forge a contract with another entity** roll+CHA on a 10+ your contract is successful and you set the terms. Though it is a favorable contract on your end something must attract the other signee, the GM will have the last say as to what is a deal breaker here. On a 7-9 the contract does not particularly favor either party but both see potential for benefit. On a 6- the contract favors the other party, the GM will tell you how and you can refuse to accept, but you are likely to spark some dissent.
# Gear
Your Load is 12+STR
- Shortsword
- Dungeon rations (5 uses)
- Shield (+1 armor when equipped)
# Bonds
Fill in the name of one of your companions in at least one:
\_\_\_\_ could be of use to me, we should forge a contract.
\_\_\_\_ has fought beside me bravely, I can trust them.
\_\_\_\_ is already part of my web.
# Advanced Moves
**When you gain a level from 2-5, choose from these moves.**
### Fearsome Deal Broker
When you Tug at the Threads or Forge a Contract you may use STR instead of CHA
### I Want That
Mark something on or owned by someone. You gain +1 any time you work to acquiring it. You can only mark one thing at a time and must wait 24 hours before you can mark a new thing. Upon acquiring the something you've marked you get +1 to whatever move is made or involves it the first time it is used.
### Come at Me
When you **defend by calling attention to yourself in battle** you may use STR instead of CON. 
### Heart of a Fighter
Choose one move from the fighter class list.

---
**When you gain a level from 6-10, choose from these moves.**
### That *WILL* Be Mine
*Requires I Want That*
Both bonuses for I Want That become +2 and the bonus upon requirement can be used one additional time. You must now wait two days to change your mark, your obsession is increased.
### Now Things are Interesting
You have an ongiong +1 to Hack and Slash when you **go into combat outnumbered or outclassed**
